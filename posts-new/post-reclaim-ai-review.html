Reclaim AI Review: Is It Worth It for ADHD?

Reclaim AI is a calendar tool that books your tasks, habits, focus time and breaks around your meetings, then moves them when the day changes. It has a free plan and paid plans from $10 a month, and it suits people who live in Google Calendar or Outlook on a computer.

  • Try Reclaim if a busy work calendar keeps eating your focus time, and you would rather have blocks placed for you than plan by hand.
  • Skip it if you plan mainly on your phone, or schedules that move on their own make you anxious.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if the block is on your calendar and you still cannot start, and you want the task split into timed steps.

Is Reclaim AI good for ADHD?

It can be, especially if manual time blocking is the part you never finish. It was not built for ADHD, and it schedules the work rather than helping you begin it.

Reclaim did publish a 2021 post ranking itself first among ADHD tools, which is marketing rather than evidence. Saner.AI's calendar roundup also rates it well for ADHD, though Saner is a competitor, and the G2 review page we read did not mention ADHD.

The reasoning makes sense as far as it goes. Planning a week draws on executive function, the skills behind organizing and following through, and ADHD can make that harder, so handing part of it to software may take some load off.

What does Reclaim AI get right for ADHD brains?

Time blocking you do not have to do: Blocks appear without you dragging anything, and they move when the day moves. If rebuilding a plan after one meeting makes you give up on it, that may be the main appeal.

Time you can see: Tasks and habits sit on your calendar with real lengths, which may help with time blindness, trouble sensing how long things take (our time blindness guide goes deeper).

Breaks that get booked: Buffer time adds breaks and travel time automatically. That can soften the jump between tasks, which our guide to ADHD transitions explains.

A free plan worth trying: Lite lets you test the idea at no cost, with one habit and a one-week scheduling range. One week is short, but it is enough to learn whether moving blocks help you or stress you out.

Where does Reclaim AI fall short?

No native phone app: Carly's review and G2 reviewers both report this, and Reclaim's mobile guide points you to a home screen web app or your calendar app. If your phone is where you plan, that is a real gap.

A block is not a start: A calendar can tell you it is time to write the report, and you can still sit frozen in front of it. Blocks that move themselves can also make it easier to let them slide.

Setup takes some thinking: Priorities, hours and scheduling rules all need configuring, and G2 reviewers mention a learning curve. That first setup session can be its own hurdle.

Reported glitches: Carly's review collects complaints about Focus Time entries reappearing after deletion and calendar sync lag. Carly sells a competing product, so treat that list as one-sided.

Work IT can block it: Carly's review also says many corporate Outlook accounts need IT approval before Reclaim can connect. Check that before you build a routine around it.

How much does Reclaim AI cost?

Lite is free for one user, and Reclaim's plan guide lists its limits. Starter is $10 per seat a month billed yearly or $12 monthly, and Business is $15 or $18, according to its pricing page when we last read it on October 9, 2026.

Starter stretches the scheduling range to 8 weeks with unlimited habits, and Business goes to 12 weeks. Its plan guide also mentions education and nonprofit discounts, and its site has shown new-user offers, so check the price you are shown.

Is Reclaim AI worth it?

The free plan is worth trying if your calendar is the problem, since it costs nothing to find out. Pay only if you hit the limits and the blocks are getting done.

Give it two real work weeks and count finished blocks, not booked ones. If they keep sliding, the schedule was not what was stopping you, and our piece on why ADHD planner apps get abandoned may sound familiar.

How is Reclaim AI different from Sunsama and Motion?

Reclaim fills your calendar for you, while Sunsama walks you through planning by hand each morning (our Sunsama review). If you skip rituals, lean Reclaim; if you need to feel in charge of your day, lean Sunsama.

Motion works on the same auto-scheduling idea, and our Motion alternatives guide compares the two. For more tools of this type, see our AI calendar guide.

How is Reclaim AI different from FOCO?

Reclaim decides when a task should happen. FOCO, the app we make, helps once that moment arrives and you cannot begin.

That gap is task initiation, the step between knowing what to do and doing it (our guide to task initiation deficit explains it). In FOCO, AI splits the task into small steps, each with a time estimate, and a focus timer runs with an AI body-doubling companion on screen.

Reclaim is the stronger choice for defending time on a work calendar, and it works on a computer while FOCO is an iPhone and Android app. Some people could use both.

  • You never finish time blocking by hand, or you give up when the day changes.

FAQ

Is Reclaim AI free?

Yes, there is a free Lite plan for one user. It limits you to a 1-week scheduling range, 1 habit and up to 2 connected calendars.

How much does Reclaim AI cost per month?

Starter is $10 a seat billed yearly or $12 monthly, and Business is $15 or $18, as of our last check of its pricing page on October 9, 2026.

Does Reclaim AI have a mobile app?

Not a native one, according to the reviews we read. Reclaim's guide suggests adding the web app to your home screen or using your phone's calendar app.

Does Reclaim AI work with Outlook?

Yes. It works with Google Calendar and Outlook, though some corporate Outlook accounts reportedly need IT approval first.

Is Reclaim AI owned by Dropbox?

Yes. TechCrunch reported the acquisition in August 2024, and Reclaim's site now reads "from Dropbox."

Is Reclaim AI better than Motion?

Not overall, since they suit similar people. Reclaim has a free plan and a lower starting price, and it stays inside your existing calendar.
