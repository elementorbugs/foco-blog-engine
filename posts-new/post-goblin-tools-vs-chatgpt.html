What do the two tools actually look like?

Goblin Tools is a plain page with one box. We tested it on goblin.tools on October 9, 2026: "Clean the kitchen" became six sensible steps within seconds, each one a checkbox.

ChatGPT is a conversation. We did not test it for this comparison; the screens below are OpenAI's official App Store images, one of which shows someone saying they feel overwhelmed and the other asking for "a few simple steps".

Which is better for ADHD task breakdown?

For a single stuck task, Goblin Tools. It removes the decisions: you type the task, tap once and get steps, with nothing to phrase or set up.

That matters because starting leans on executive function, the brain skills behind planning and sequencing (our executive function guide explains more). Writing a good prompt is itself a small planning task.

For a tangled task, ChatGPT. If "do my taxes" depends on missing documents, a deadline and how tired you are, you can say all of that and get steps that fit.

How much effort does each one take to use?

Goblin Tools takes almost none. The page opens straight to a box, and the website needed no account in our test.

ChatGPT asks more of you. You decide how to phrase the request, read a longer answer and often ask again to make the steps smaller.

A general chat can also drift. One question about the dishes can turn into twenty minutes of unrelated questions, which is the opposite of starting.

Which gives better steps?

It depends on the task. For everyday chores, Goblin Tools' steps were practical and the right size in our test, and the chili setting makes them smaller or larger.

For anything personal or unusual, ChatGPT can do better because it knows what you told it. Goblin Tools only sees one line of text.

Both can be wrong. Goblin Tools' own About page says its results are "only guesswork", and a step that does not fit your situation is worth skipping or rewording (Goblin Tools About).

How do you get small steps out of ChatGPT?

Be specific about size and the first move. Vague requests tend to come back as long lists of big steps.

A prompt that works well for task breakdown is: "Break clean the kitchen into 5 steps of under 10 minutes each. Make step one something I can do in 2 minutes. Keep it short."

Which is cheaper?

Both cost nothing to try. Goblin Tools' website is free, and its creator says existing features "will never be locked behind a paywall"; the mobile app is $0.99 once on the US App Store, with an optional Pro subscription for advanced features.

ChatGPT has a free plan, and its paid plans on the US App Store are Go at $8.00, Plus at $19.99 and Pro from $100 a month (ChatGPT on the App Store).

Does either one help you actually start?

Only up to the list. Both hand you steps, and then you are on your own with the first one.

FAQ

Is Goblin Tools better than ChatGPT?

For breaking one stuck task into steps fast, yes. For tasks that need context or follow-up questions, ChatGPT is more flexible.

Does Goblin Tools use ChatGPT?

Goblin Tools says it uses AI models from multiple providers, both open and closed source, without naming them. So it is not simply ChatGPT with a different look.

Is Goblin Tools free?

The website is free. The mobile app is a one-time $0.99 on the US App Store, and an optional Pro subscription adds advanced features.

Can ChatGPT break down tasks for ADHD?

Yes, if you ask clearly. Ask for a set number of short steps and a tiny first step, or the answer can come back as a long list.

Which is better for ADHD, Goblin Tools or ChatGPT?

Goblin Tools asks fewer decisions of you, which helps on low-energy days. ChatGPT helps more when the task is complicated or personal.
