Brainway App Review: Price, Pros, Cons and ADHD Fit

Short answer: Brainway is a subscription app for procrastination: you log your mood, sleep, habits and focus each day, and it charts which ones line up with good and bad days, plus short lessons and 14-day experiments. It is free to download, but you need a subscription to keep using it, and several reviews describe billing problems.

  • Try Brainway if you like logging and want to see whether sleep, caffeine or screen time track with your worst procrastination days.
  • Skip it if daily logging already slips for you, or a subscription that is hard to cancel would cost you more stress than it saves.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if the problem is starting the task in front of you right now, and you want it split into small timed steps.

What does Brainway look like in use?

We did not test Brainway hands-on. The screens below are its official App Store screenshots, so they show the app as the developer presents it.

The core of the app is a set of correlations: you log factors such as caffeine, junk food, screen time or a late bedtime, and an "Effect on Procrastination" card shows each one as a plus or minus percentage.

Other charts plot two metrics against each other, such as mood and sleep.

The Programs tab holds short reading lessons and experiments, such as a 14-day exercise experiment. Brainway can also pull steps, sleep and other health data from Apple Health or Google Fit.

Is Brainway good for ADHD?

It is not built for ADHD, and its listings never mention it. Brainway is a general procrastination and habit tracker, and its website says its lessons use "CBT-based practices."

That idea has some support: in a 2010 JAMA trial, adults with ADHD on medication who got cognitive behavioral therapy from a therapist improved more than a comparison group. An app that offers CBT-style lessons is not the same as that therapy, and Brainway does not claim it is.

The bigger question is fit.

ADHD affects executive function, the skills behind planning and follow-through, as Russell Barkley's fact sheet explains. An app that only pays off after weeks of daily logging asks for exactly the steady routine many ADHD brains find hardest.

What does Brainway get right?

  • It treats procrastination as more than laziness. Research backs that up: a large review of procrastination studies linked it most strongly to how unpleasant a task feels, impulsiveness and distractibility.
  • Patterns you can act on: If late nights really do line up with your worst days, seeing it on a chart can be more convincing than being told.
  • Small experiments: Trying one change for 14 days is gentler than a full life overhaul.
  • Less typing: Health data can flow in from Apple Health or Google Fit, so not every metric has to be entered by hand.

Where might Brainway fall short?

It needs a subscription to keep going: The App Store listing says "continued access requires a subscription." Its own pages do not say what you can do without one.

Billing complaints: Several reviews on its US App Store page say they were charged after canceling, or charged on a different schedule than they expected, and the developer replied to them.

Google Play shows 2.7 stars against 4.1 on the App Store.

Insights take time: One App Store reviewer found that an experiment needs at least 3 days of logging without it and 3 days with it before any effect shows. Missed logging days push that back.

Tracking is not starting: Brainway can show that bad sleep goes with bad days. It does not help you take the first step on the task that is stuck today, which is task initiation, a separate problem.

How much does Brainway cost?

On the US App Store, Brainway lists two "Unlimited access" in-app purchases, at $29.99 and $59.99. The store does not say how long each one lasts.

The website sells plans after a "2-minute procrastination test," and its subscription terms mention weekly, monthly and yearly billing, free trials and introductory prices that "may revert to standard pricing." We could not get the quiz to load, so we do not know the web prices. Before you pay, check the exact price and the renewal period on the payment screen.

How to cancel depends on where you paid: through Apple or Google, cancel in your account's subscription settings. On the website, use Brainway's Manage Subscription page or email its support team before the period ends, according to its terms.

How much does Brainway cost?

How do I cancel my Brainway subscription?

If you paid through Apple or Google, cancel in that account's subscription settings. If you paid on the website, use Brainway's Manage Subscription page or email its support team before the period ends.

Is Brainway good for ADHD?

It is not made for ADHD and does not claim to be. It may help if you like tracking, but it asks for daily logging and does not help you start a task.

Does Brainway use CBT?

Its website says its lessons use CBT-based practices and cognitive reframing. It is not therapy, and it does not replace a clinician.
