AppBlock Review: Is the App Blocker Free and Safe?

Short answer: AppBlock is a phone app blocker for Android and iPhone, with browser extensions, made by the Czech developer MobileSoft, free with limits that Premium removes. Its Strict Mode is the reason people pick it, because it makes a running block hard to switch off.

  • Try AppBlock if your phone is the main distraction and you want schedules, daily limits and a lock you cannot undo on impulse.
  • Skip it if you need to block a whole Windows or Mac computer, or if sharing data with advertisers is a deal breaker for you.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if the apps are blocked and you still cannot start, and you want one task split into small timed steps.

What does AppBlock look like in use?

We did not test AppBlock hands-on. The screens below are from its official App Store listing, so they show the app the way the developer presents it.

When you open a blocked app, you get a "Blocked by AppBlock" screen with a count of how often you tried. Blocks run as a one-tap Quick Block or as schedules, and Strict Mode shows a countdown until the lock ends.

You can build a block four main ways: a time schedule, a limit on how many times you open an app, a daily usage limit, or a location. An Insights tab shows screen time by day and your most used apps.

Is AppBlock free?

Yes, with limits. AppBlock is free to download, and its help center says plainly that you can use it for free.

The free plan caps how much you can set up. On the official pricing page, the comparison table lists free limits such as two or three schedules, a daily usage limit of up to 30 minutes, and Strict Mode of up to 12 or 24 hours, depending on the platform tab.

Premium adds features the free plan lacks, including Pomodoro and timer options for Quick Block, pausing a schedule, allowlist blocking and extending a running Strict Mode timer.

How much does AppBlock Premium cost?

There is no single price. The US App Store listing showed several Premium options on November 12, 2026: yearly plans at $29.99, $44.99 and $59.99, and monthly plans at $4.99 and $11.99.

The price you see depends on the offer inside the app, so check it before you subscribe. The pricing page also lists a one-time lifetime plan, but it did not show a price when we checked.

According to AppBlock's help center, the 7-day trial is for people who have never subscribed. On Android it renews into the yearly plan unless you cancel before it ends.

How strict is AppBlock?

As strict as you set it. The help center says Strict Mode stops you from turning off active blocks, editing or deleting blocking rules, and uninstalling AppBlock.

You also choose how a Strict Mode session can end:

  • Timer: the lock stays on until the time runs out.
  • Follow schedules: the lock matches your blocking schedule (Premium only on iPhone).
  • Cooldown: you can unlock, but only after a set wait.
  • Approval: a trusted person has to approve the unlock request (Premium only on iPhone).
  • PIN or charger: you need a code or have to plug in your phone.

The help center suggests PIN or charger for your first try, so you can get out if something feels wrong. For real emergencies, support also describes an emergency unblocking route, which can end with a one-time unlock code.

On iPhone, AppBlock uses Apple's Screen Time API. Apple does not let blockers lock the Settings app directly, and AppBlock's help center says its Shortcuts workaround is no longer reliable from iOS 26.

Is AppBlock safe?

It is a mainstream app sold through Apple and Google, made by MobileSoft s.r.o., and it has been on the App Store since 2021. The question is mostly about data.

The App Store privacy label says identifiers may be used to track you across other companies' apps and sites, and lists third-party advertising among the uses of data not linked to you. Google Play's data safety section says the app may share personal and financial info with third parties, encrypts data in transit, and lets you request deletion.

The developer says its iPhone blocking runs through Apple's Screen Time API and that your personal data is never shared. If tracking matters to you, read the App Store privacy label yourself before you install.

Is AppBlock good for ADHD?

It can help with one part of ADHD: acting on the urge to open a feed. ADHD is linked to differences in executive function, the skills behind self-control and planning, as Russell Barkley's fact sheet explains.

A block you set while calm, and cannot undo while distracted, moves the decision to a better moment. The cooldown and approval options do the same thing more gently.

It is a tool, not a treatment. For questions about ADHD itself, the National Institute of Mental Health is a better place to start.

Where might AppBlock fall short?

The free plan is tight: two or three schedules and a short Strict Mode cover a simple routine, but heavier use will push you toward Premium.

Pricing is hard to pin down: with several yearly and monthly prices on the listing, you cannot know your price until the app shows you an offer.

Weaker on iPhone: Apple's rules limit what any blocker can lock, and AppBlock says Settings blocking is no longer reliable from iOS 26. On Android, Strict Mode can block device settings.

Blocking is not starting: with every app locked, you can still sit in front of a task you cannot begin. That is task initiation, a separate problem from distraction.

FAQ

Is AppBlock free?

Yes, AppBlock is free to download and use, with limits on schedules, usage limits and Strict Mode length. Premium removes those limits.

How much is AppBlock Premium?

The US App Store listed yearly plans from $29.99 to $59.99 and monthly plans from $4.99 to $11.99 on November 12, 2026. The price you are offered in the app can vary.

Is AppBlock safe?

It is a mainstream app from MobileSoft s.r.o., sold through Apple and Google. Its App Store privacy label says identifiers may be used to track you, so read the label if that matters to you.

Can you turn off AppBlock Strict Mode early?

Normally only through the unlock method you chose, such as a timer, cooldown, PIN, charger or a trusted person's approval. For real emergencies, AppBlock support describes an emergency unblocking route.

Does AppBlock work on iPhone?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and on Apple Watch, using Apple's Screen Time API. Some locks are weaker than on Android, because Apple limits what blockers can control.

Does AppBlock have a free trial?

Yes, a 7-day Premium trial for people who have never subscribed. On Android it renews into the yearly plan unless you cancel before it ends.
