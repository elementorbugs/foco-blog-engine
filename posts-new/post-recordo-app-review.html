Recordo App Review: An AI ADHD Planner With Chat Coaches

Short answer: Recordo is an ADHD planner where you talk or type your thoughts into a chat, and AI turns them into tasks, reminders and notes, with coach personalities and calendar views on top. It is young, with few US ratings so far, and Pro costs $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year.

  • Try Recordo if your main problem is capture: thoughts arrive faster than you can file them, and talking feels easier than filling in forms.
  • Skip it if you want a long public track record before you trust an app with your planning, or you need to see the free limits up front.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if capture is fine but starting is not, and you want one stuck task split into timed steps with a focus companion on screen.

What does Recordo look like in use?

We did not test Recordo hands-on. The screens below are from its official App Store images, so they show the app as the developer presents it.

You tell the coach what is on your mind, and it replies with saved tasks and due dates. The home screen then shows one next task, with buttons to complete it, skip it or ask the coach for help.

Behind that sit routines, color-coded projects and a calendar that shows tasks as time blocks. It covers a lot more than capture, which is worth weighing if long feature lists tend to wear you out.

Is Recordo built for ADHD?

Yes, by its own description. The developer says they built it because they have ADHD and "every other planner felt like homework," and the setup asks about struggles such as "Hard to start" and "Time slips."

That focus makes sense, since ADHD is linked to trouble with executive function, the skills behind planning and following through. Still, Recordo is a planning tool and not a treatment, and the National Institute of Mental Health is a better place to start for questions about ADHD itself.

What does Recordo get right for ADHD brains?

Capture without forms: You can talk, type or send a photo, and AI sorts it into a task, a reminder or a note. If a brain dump usually stalls at the filing stage, that removes a step (our ADHD brain dump guide explains the method).

One next task: The home screen shows a single next step instead of the whole list, with skip and remind-later buttons. That suits days when a long list is the thing stopping you.

Finding things later: You can search past entries in plain language instead of remembering where you saved them. Saner.AI and MindFlow also play in this area, so those reviews are useful for comparison.

Sharing with family: Projects can be shared, and the listing says invited people join for free, can check tasks off and get reminders.

Scheduled automations: Scheduled runs can plan your day or prepare for tomorrow, and the coach can remember details about you. The listing says that memory can be turned off.

Where might Recordo fall short?

A short track record: It first appeared on December 16, 2025, and has only 8 US App Store ratings, so a 4.9 tells you little yet. The fuller signal is Google Play, with 4.65 from 336 ratings and about 28K installs, as reported by AppGoblin.

Unclear free limits: The listings do not say what the free version includes. A recent update moved photo sending and custom coach personalities to free, so the line is also still moving.

Fast-moving software: New versions ship several times a month, which is good for fixes and means things can change under you. A late October update fixed the app closing right after opening on iOS 27.

Limited outside evidence: The reviews we could read were the ones the developer quotes in its own listing. We found no independent hands-on reviews, so treat the praise as promising rather than proven.

Voice and photo data: Its privacy labels list audio data and photos or videos as data linked to you, used for analytics and app functionality. The description also states no ads and no data selling, so read the policy if that matters to you.

How much does Recordo cost?

Recordo is free to download, with Pro as an in-app purchase. On the US App Store, Pro is $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year, and Pro Family is $99.99.

The listing does not say how long Family lasts or whether a trial applies, so check the paywall in your region before you pay.

Is Recordo worth it?

It could be if capture is your bottleneck, because the free download lets you find out cheaply. Use it for a week of real, messy days before you pay.

Watch one thing during that week: whether tasks get done, or only get neatly saved. Many apps feel great in the first few days, and our piece on why ADHD planner apps get abandoned covers what tends to happen next.

How is Recordo different from FOCO?

Recordo is built around capture and a chat coach across your whole life admin. FOCO, the app we make, is built around the moment you cannot start one task.

That gap is task initiation, the step between knowing what to do and doing it (our guide to task initiation deficit explains it). In FOCO, AI splits the task into small steps, each with a time estimate, and a focus timer runs with an AI body-doubling companion on screen.

The prices are close, and both apps offer voice capture and a calendar. Recordo also lists shared projects, Google Calendar and Todoist import, so it is the broader tool on paper.

FAQ

Is Recordo free?

Recordo is free to download and has a free version, with Pro as a paid upgrade. Its listings do not say exactly what the free version includes.

How much is Recordo Pro?

On the US App Store, Pro is $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year. Pro Family is listed at $99.99.

Is Recordo good for ADHD?

It is designed for ADHD and looks best suited to people whose main struggle is capturing thoughts. It is still young, with little independent review so far, so test it on real days first.

Is Recordo on Android?

Yes. It is on Google Play as well as the App Store, with about 28K installs on Android according to AppGoblin.

What are the Recordo coach personalities?

There are three built-in styles: The Pilot breaks tasks into tiny steps, The Buddy is a warm companion, and The Hype turns chores into a game. Custom personalities are also available.

Can I move my tasks from Todoist to Recordo?

Yes. The listings say open tasks and projects can be imported from Todoist.
