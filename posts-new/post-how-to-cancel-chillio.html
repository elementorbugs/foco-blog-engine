Short answer: Cancel Chillio where you paid: in iPhone Settings (tap your name, then Subscriptions), in Google Play under Subscriptions, or by logging in at get-chillio.app and tapping Cancel subscription (or emailing support@get-chillio.app). Do it at least 24 hours before renewal, and know that deleting the app cancels nothing.

How do you know where you paid for Chillio?

Check your email first. A "receipt from Apple" means the App Store, a Google Play order email means Google, and a payment email after a quiz on get-chillio.app means you paid Chillio directly through its website.

This matters because a website subscription never shows up in your iPhone's subscription list. Apple says that if you can't find a subscription there, "you might've bought the subscription from another company," and you need to cancel with that company (Apple Support).

If your email gives no clue, look at your bank or card statement. Apple suggests the same thing to find out who is billing you.

How do you cancel Chillio on iPhone?

If you subscribed inside the iPhone app, Apple bills you, so you cancel in Settings. These are Apple's steps:

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Chillio.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. You may need to scroll down to see it.

If there is no Cancel button, or you see an expiration message in red, Apple says the subscription is already canceled. To ask for money back, use Apple's refund request page, since Apple, not Chillio, handles App Store refunds.

How do you cancel Chillio on Android?

If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel there. Google's steps are short:

  1. Open Subscriptions in Google Play.
  2. Select Chillio.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription.
  4. Follow the instructions.

Google is clear that "when you uninstall the app, your subscription won't cancel," and that you keep access for the time you've already paid (Google Play Help). If Chillio isn't listed, you may have subscribed with a different Google account.

How do you cancel a Chillio subscription you bought on the website?

Log in at get-chillio.app with the same email and password you used when you signed up, then cancel from your account. That is what Chillio's own "Manage your subscription" page tells users to do.

Chillio's terms offer a second route: email support@get-chillio.app at least 24 hours before the trial or billing period ends. Chillio's Terms of Use say subscription requests must come from the email you use to log in, so send it from that inbox (see also its Subscription and Refund Terms).

A short email is enough: "Please cancel my Chillio subscription and stop all future charges. Please confirm in writing." Add the date you signed up and the last 4 digits of your card, if you have them.

What happens when you tap Cancel subscription?

Chillio asks you to stay twice before it cancels. We read the button text on its website, and the flow runs like this:

  • First screen: it asks if you're sure you want to give up on your goal and lists what you'll lose. Tap "Continue to cancel," not "Keep my plan."
  • Second screen: it asks for your main reason. Any answer moves you on.
  • Third screen: "A small gift from us" offers a free month. Tapping "Claim free month" keeps your full access active, so you are still subscribed.
  • At the end: your account should show "Your subscription is cancelled." Take a screenshot of it, and of the "Active until" date.

We didn't buy a plan, so we couldn't click through it ourselves, and your screens may look a little different. If the button fails, the page itself says to try again or contact support.

Can you get a refund from Chillio?

Only in narrow cases for website payments. Chillio's terms call fees "non-refundable" except under a money-back guarantee with strict conditions.

  • Timing: you must ask within 30 days of your first purchase and before your billing period ends.
  • No results: the guarantee is for people who did not get "recognizable changes/results."
  • Proof of use: you must have followed the program for 6 days in a row in the first 30 days (4 days in the first 7 for weekly plans), and you must send screenshots of completed lessons.
  • Exclusions: the guarantee doesn't cover simply not liking the product, or not realizing the trial would turn into a paid plan.
  • Add-ons: extras bought at checkout, such as wellness guides, are generally final.

Local law can add rights. The same terms mention a 14-day cooling-off period for UK users, the EU's 14-day right of withdrawal (which the terms say you give up once the purchase is delivered, unless the content is defective), and a note that California users can request a refund under state consumer protection law.

App Store and Google Play payments follow Apple's and Google's refund rules instead. Send those requests to the store, not to Chillio.

What if Chillio keeps charging you after you cancel?

Email support@get-chillio.app from the address you paid with, and attach your cancellation screenshot. On Trustpilot, Chillio replies to most negative reviews, and some of those reviews describe charges after canceling.

If a website charge still goes through, call your card company. For credit cards, the FTC says your written dispute should reach the issuer within 60 days after the first bill with the error was sent to you (FTC).

FAQ

Does deleting the Chillio app cancel my subscription?

No. Chillio's terms say uninstalling the app won't cancel your subscription, so cancel in the App Store, in Google Play or on the website.

How do I cancel my Chillio free trial?

Cancel the same way as a paid plan, at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Otherwise, Chillio's terms say you'll be charged the price shown on the payment screen for the plan you chose.

Can I cancel Chillio by email?

Yes, if you paid on the website. Email support@get-chillio.app from the address you used to buy, at least 24 hours before the billing period ends, and ask for written confirmation.

Why can't I find Chillio in my iPhone subscriptions?

You probably paid on Chillio's website, not in the App Store. Apple only lists subscriptions it bills, so log in at get-chillio.app or email Chillio support instead.

Will I lose access to Chillio right away?

Usually not. Google says you keep access for the time you paid, and your Chillio web account lists an "Active until" date.

Can I get my money back from Chillio?

App Store and Google Play refunds go through Apple or Google. Website refunds need Chillio's money-back guarantee, which requires asking within 30 days and proof you followed the program.
