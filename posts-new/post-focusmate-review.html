What is Focusmate and how does it work?

Focusmate is a website for virtual coworking: you book a time, get matched with another member, and work side by side on a video call. Each of you says what you'll do at the start and how it went at the end.

STEP 1

Book a slot

Pick 25, 50 or 75 minutes, or use Focus Now to match right away.

STEP 2

Say your goal

A quick hello and one line on what you'll get done.

STEP 3

Work, then check in

Cameras on, mics usually off, and a short check-in at the end.

Its FAQ says over 99% of bookings get matched with a partner. You can favorite people you liked working with, and its Quiet Mode matches you with others who'd rather not talk.

What is body doubling, and why does it help with ADHD?

Body doubling means doing a task while another person is present, in the room or on a screen. Many people with ADHD say it makes starting and staying on a boring task much easier.

The research is still thin: there are no large controlled trials of body doubling yet. The idea fits what we know about ADHD and executive function, the brain skills for starting and sticking with tasks (NIMH), since another person adds gentle outside structure.

Focusmate's FAQ says "many Focusmate members have ADHD" and cites its own survey: neurodivergent members report a 161% productivity boost on average. That's a self-reported company figure, not an independent study.

Do you need a camera, and is Focusmate safe?

Yes, a camera is required and you're asked to keep video on for the whole session; the microphone is optional. That's the part that makes it work for some people and rules it out for others.

For safety, you can report a partner from the session card, block or snooze people, and set a gender preference for who you're matched with. Late arrivals and no-shows lower a member's timeliness score, and accounts can be frozen for breaking the community guidelines.

Does Focusmate have an app?

No, its FAQ says "we don't currently have an app," so you use it in a browser on a computer, phone or tablet. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Where does Focusmate fall short?

The most common complaint is partners who switch or don't show up. On Trustpilot, where it scores 4.9 from 351 reviews, one member wrote that "the only annoyance is when you are matched but then the partner changes, or there is a no-show."

What should you do once your session starts?

Name one small, specific first step before the call begins, because "work on my thesis" is too big to start in 50 minutes. Freezing at the start of a session is often ADHD task paralysis, not laziness.

That's the job FOCO is built for. Its AI turns a stuck task into small timed steps, everything you type, say or snap lands in its built-in calendar, and its Focus Mode keeps an AI companion on screen with calm music while you work.

A simple combo: break the task down in FOCO, then say step one as your goal at the start of a Focusmate session. For solo work, a 25-minute timer and the Pomodoro method work the same way.
