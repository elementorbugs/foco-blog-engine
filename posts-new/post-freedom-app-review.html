Freedom App Review: Can One Blocker Cover Every Device?

Freedom is an app and website blocker that blocks distractions on your phone, tablet and computer in one session, on Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android and Chromebook. The free plan blocks apps, sites or the whole internet, and Premium adds scheduling, Locked Mode and long sessions for US$39.99 a year, US$8.99 a month or a one-time Forever plan.

  • Try Freedom if your distractions jump from phone to laptop, and you want one blocker that covers both.
  • Skip it if you only need to block one device, where a cheaper or free tool may do.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if the distractions are blocked and you still cannot start, and you want the task split into small timed steps.

What does Freedom look like in use?

We did not test Freedom hands-on. The screens below are its official App Store screenshots, so they show the app as the developer presents it.

You start a session, pick a blocklist and the devices it covers, and set a length. When you try to open a blocked app, Freedom shows a calm green screen that says "You are free. Go do great things."

Premium lets sessions start on their own, such as "Deep Work Mornings" on weekdays. Blocklists can mix phone apps, Mac apps and websites, and Focus Sounds play café or nature audio while you work.

Is Freedom good for ADHD?

It can be, especially if your attention slips from one screen to another. Close Instagram on your phone, and it is one tab away on your laptop, which is exactly the gap Freedom's synced sessions close.

ADHD is linked to differences in executive function, including stopping an impulse, as Russell Barkley's fact sheet explains. A schedule you set while calm takes that decision out of the moment.

Locked Mode goes further: after one minute, the session cannot be ended and uninstalling is locked. That helps if you tend to cancel blocks, and it can feel stressful if you rarely plan that far ahead.

A blocker removes distractions, but it does not help you start. That is task initiation, which our guide to task initiation deficit explains.

For ADHD itself, see the National Institute of Mental Health.

Where might Freedom fall short?

The best parts are Premium: Scheduling, recurring sessions and Locked Mode all need Premium, so the free plan is mostly for manual sessions.

Softer than a hard lock by default: Without Locked Mode, you can end a session early. Cold Turkey Blocker is built to be stricter on computers.

Some features are desktop only: Website exceptions, where you allow only a few sites and block the rest, work on Mac and Windows only.

Sale prices change: The Forever plan was half price when we checked, so check the current price before you decide.

How much does Freedom cost?

The free plan costs nothing. Premium is US$39.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or US$8.99 a month, according to its App Store listing and pricing page.

The Forever plan is one payment with no subscription, listed at US$199 and shown at US$99.50 on sale on November 14, 2026. Prices outside the US may differ.

FAQ

Is Freedom free?

Yes, there is a free plan that blocks apps, websites or the whole internet across unlimited devices. Scheduling, recurring sessions and Locked Mode need Premium.

How much is Freedom Premium?

Premium is US$39.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or US$8.99 a month. A one-time Forever plan lists at US$199 and was on sale for US$99.50 on November 14, 2026.

What is Freedom's Locked Mode?

Locked Mode is a Premium feature that stops you from ending a session early. You get one minute to undo it, and after that the session cannot be ended and logging out or uninstalling is locked.

Does Freedom work on Android and Windows?

Yes. Freedom runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, Android and Chromebook, and one session can cover all of them.

Is Freedom better than Cold Turkey?

Freedom is better if you need your phone and computer blocked together. Cold Turkey is stricter on Windows and Mac, has no phone version, and costs a one-time US$45 for Pro.

Is Freedom good for ADHD?

It can help if distractions jump between your devices, and scheduled sessions take the decision out of the moment. It does not help you start a task, so pair it with another tool if that is the hard part.
