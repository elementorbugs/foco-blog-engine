Cold Turkey Blocker Review: Strict Blocking for ADHD

Cold Turkey Blocker is a Windows and Mac app that blocks websites, apps or your whole computer, and its locks make a block very hard to undo once started. The free version blocks websites, and Pro is a one-time US$45 that adds app blocking, schedules, breaks and every lock type.

  • Try Cold Turkey if you keep switching off softer blockers, and your distractions live on a laptop or desktop.
  • Skip it if your phone is the main problem, since there is no Android or iOS version, or if a hard lock would make you anxious.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if the distractions are gone and you still cannot start, and you want one task split into small timed steps.

What does Cold Turkey Blocker look like in use?

We did not test Cold Turkey hands-on. The screens below come from its official website and user guide, so they show the app as the developer presents it.

You build a block list, start it, then pick a lock. Once it is locked, a blocked site shows a plain "This website is blocked" page with a quote instead of the feed you were reaching for.

Blocks can also follow a weekly schedule, such as mornings fully blocked and afternoons with 60 minutes of allowed time. A statistics tab shows where your hours went, site by site.

How strict is Cold Turkey Blocker?

Very strict, by design. The homepage says it "makes it almost impossible to stop the block once you lock it," and adds: "No, you can't just uninstall it."

According to the user guide, a locked block makes the uninstaller fail, blocks your browser until the extension is turned back on, and closes common gaps such as guest browsing.

You choose how hard the lock is:

  • Timer: no changes until a set date and time, and you can extend it while it runs.
  • Random text: you must type out 1 to 999 random characters to unlock.
  • Time range: changes are only allowed during certain hours.
  • Restart: you must restart the computer to unlock a continuous block.
  • Password: a Pro option, useful if a partner or parent holds the password.

There is also Frozen Turkey, which locks, logs off or shuts down the whole computer for a set time.

Is Cold Turkey Blocker good for ADHD?

It can help with one part of ADHD: acting on the impulse to open a tab. ADHD is linked to differences in executive function, the skills behind self-control and planning, as Russell Barkley's fact sheet explains.

A block that you set while calm and cannot undo while distracted moves the decision to a better moment. That is the case for a strict blocker over a gentle reminder.

The flexible breaks matter too. An all-or-nothing block can feel punishing, while an allowance or a short Pomodoro break leaves room for a normal day (our Pomodoro guide for ADHD covers that rhythm).

It is a tool, not a treatment. The National Institute of Mental Health is a better place to start for questions about ADHD itself.

Where might Cold Turkey Blocker fall short?

No phone version: The developer says phone systems prevent an app from being hard to delete, so there is none. If your phone is the problem, see our reviews of Opal, Brick and Bloom.

Strict cuts both ways: A long lock set on an optimistic morning can block a site you truly need later. Start with short timer locks until you know how you use it.

Admin rights required: The app cannot be installed without them, which rules out many work laptops.

Blocking is not starting: With every distraction gone, you can still sit in front of a blank document. That is task initiation, a separate problem from distraction.

Email-only support: The developer works alone and says replies take one to two business days.

How do you uninstall Cold Turkey Blocker?

If no block is locked, you remove it like any other app on Windows or Mac. If a block is locked, the uninstaller is designed to fail until the lock ends.

Cold Turkey's support page links official reinstall instructions, which appear after a 69-second wait. The developer says the wait stops you from switching off the extension just to read them, if you blocked that page.

If you are stuck, the developer's support page has a contact form. We will not share workarounds, because getting past your own lock defeats the point of the app.

FAQ

Is Cold Turkey Blocker free?

Yes, there is a free version that blocks websites for set times, with exceptions and statistics. App blocking, schedules, breaks and most lock types need Pro.

How much is Cold Turkey Blocker Pro?

Pro is a one-time US$45 on the official comparison page, checked on November 9, 2026. One purchase covers every computer you personally use, on Windows and Mac.

Is Cold Turkey Blocker safe?

It is sold directly by Cold Turkey Software, which says settings and statistics stay on your computer and that it does not collect your web history. It needs admin rights to install, so download it only from the official site.

Can you uninstall Cold Turkey Blocker during a block?

Not while the block is locked, by design. The uninstaller fails until the lock ends, and you can uninstall normally after that.

Does Cold Turkey Blocker work on iPhone or Android?

No. The developer supports only Windows and macOS, and says phone systems do not allow an app to be hard to delete.

Does Cold Turkey Blocker have a free trial?

Yes. The free version includes a 7-day trial of Pro, started with the "upgrade to pro" button in the app, and Pro has a 30-day refund guarantee.
