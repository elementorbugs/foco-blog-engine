Mellow Flow App Review: Price, Refunds and Who It Fits

Short answer: MellowFlow (also written Mellow Flow) is a web program of short lessons based on cognitive behavioral therapy, for procrastination and focus, sold by subscription. On its US checkout page on November 14, 2026, plans cost $59.99 a month, $125.98 for 3 months or $179.98 for 6 months, all auto-renewing, and refunds are narrow.

  • Try MellowFlow if you want daily reading and reflection about why you put things off, and you are fine with a subscription you must cancel yourself.
  • Skip it if you want to try before paying, need a clear refund, or want help doing today's task rather than learning about it.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if you already know why you procrastinate and need the first step of a real task made small enough to start.

What does MellowFlow look like in use?

It looks like a daily course, not a planner. Each day brings a short lesson, a quick exercise or tip, and a check-in about how your day felt, according to its Google Play listing.

The listing also describes an AI companion called Daisy, habit streaks, articles, quizzes, video and audio courses, and a private diary.

The website frames it as a program with four goals: overcome procrastination, regain focus, overcome "constant stimulation" and manage childhood trauma. You pick one, and a quiz builds your plan.

How much does MellowFlow cost?

On November 14, 2026, its US checkout listed $59.99 for 1 month, $125.98 for 3 months and $179.98 for 6 months. The 1-month plan was preselected and labeled "Top Choice for Beginners."

Prices can change. Its subscription policy says plans may start with a reduced "Initial Phase," then renew at the regular price, so the offer you see may differ.

The policy also mentions an optional one-time payment that upgrades a plan to unlimited access. Read each screen at checkout before you confirm.

Can you get a refund from MellowFlow?

Usually not. The policy says "no refunds will be issued for any paid subscription periods," with a few exceptions.

The main exception: you ask within 30 days of buying, and you send screenshots proving you completed lessons on 7 consecutive days. Not having time, or finding the course too easy or too hard, does not qualify.

To stop renewal, the policy says to cancel at least 48 hours before the next period starts, through its Contact Us form or hello@mellowflow.app. It adds that replies can take up to 30 days, with two possible 30-day extensions.

Is MellowFlow legit?

It is a real, paid service with published terms. Its terms name Moonway LLC, a Delaware company, as the seller, while the Android app is published by Solovista Limited.

Trust signals are mixed. On Trustpilot, the rating is hidden "due to a breach of our guidelines," and Trustpilot says it removed "a number of fake reviews" for the company.

Of the 1,226 Trustpilot reviews shown on November 14, 44% were 1-star and 32% were 5-star. Unhappy reviewers mostly describe charges for plans they did not mean to buy, hard cancellation and slow or automated support.

Happy reviewers say the lessons are short, well organized and insightful. We could not verify any single review either way.

Is the MellowFlow quiz a real ADHD test?

No. Its terms say the quiz and its results are not "a medical or other scientific test," and are "intended solely for entertainment purposes" and to tailor an offer.

The terms also say you must give your email address to see the quiz results. If you want to know whether you have ADHD, the National Institute of Mental Health explains how a clinician makes that diagnosis.

Is MellowFlow good for ADHD?

It may help some people understand their patterns, but it is not therapy. The Google Play listing itself says it "is not a medical service and does not replace professional care."

Cognitive behavioral therapy does have research behind it for adult ADHD. A 2010 randomized trial in JAMA found CBT improved symptoms more than relaxation training, but it used 12 one-on-one sessions with psychologists.

A self-paced lesson app is a different thing, and we found no published study of MellowFlow itself. Lessons also do not make the first step of a task smaller, which is where task initiation trouble lives, as our guide to task initiation deficit explains.

How is MellowFlow different from Wisey and Liven?

All three start with a quiz on the web and sell a subscription afterward. MellowFlow leans on CBT lessons about procrastination, while Liven is an AI life coach for stress and emotions.

Wisey sells short courses plus a timer and blocker, and its complaints follow a similar pattern about renewals. If you want a CBT-based app built for ADHD, our Inflow review covers that option.

FAQ

Is Mellow Flow legit?

It is a real paid service with published terms, sold by Moonway LLC in Delaware. Trustpilot hides its rating for a guideline breach, and many reviewers report unexpected charges, so read the plan terms before paying.

How much does the Mellow Flow app cost?

On its US checkout on November 14, 2026: $59.99 for 1 month, $125.98 for 3 months or $179.98 for 6 months, all auto-renewing. Offers can differ by funnel and over time.

Can I get a refund from Mellow Flow?

Only in narrow cases. Its policy offers one if you ask within 30 days and send screenshots showing you completed lessons on 7 consecutive days.

How do I cancel Mellow Flow?

Use the Contact Us form on mellowflow.app or email hello@mellowflow.app at least 48 hours before your next billing date. Purchases made through Google Play are cancelled in your Google Play account.

Is Mellow Flow good for ADHD?

It offers CBT-style lessons that may help some people understand procrastination, but it is not therapy and has no published study of its own. It does not break tasks into steps.

Is there a Mellow Flow app for iPhone?

Not in the App Store we checked: its old App Store page showed "can't be found" in the US, UK and Italy stores on November 14, 2026. It runs as a web app, and there is an Android app on Google Play.
