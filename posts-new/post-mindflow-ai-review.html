What does Mindflow AI look like in use?

The screenshots below are Mindflow's official App Store screenshots, not our own. We did not test Mindflow hands-on, so we describe what the listing shows and what Mindflow says the app does.

The main way in is a conversation. You switch to Live and talk, the way you'd talk to a friend, or use text chat if speaking isn't possible.

Conversations feed goal plans: areas like Task Management, Habit Change, Sleep Schedule, Time Management and Rejection Resilience, each a path of small milestones. After a chat, a summary lists key insights and the next step you agreed to.

Around that sit XP for each conversation, daily streaks, reminders, weekly summaries of your mood and progress, and pattern insights that link habits and events to your energy.

Is Mindflow AI built for ADHD?

Yes. Its listing is written entirely around ADHD, naming task paralysis, rejection sensitivity, emotional regulation, time blindness and shame among the problems it targets.

That breadth is the point: Mindflow is less a planner and more a coach that covers executive function, the brain's self-management system, together with the emotional side of ADHD, like rejection sensitive dysphoria.

What does Mindflow AI get right for ADHD brains?

It lowers the cost of asking for help. You don't need to fill in a planner first; you just say what's wrong, which works a lot like a guided brain dump.

It covers emotions, not just tasks. Overwhelm, shame and frustration often block the work more than the work itself, and Mindflow names emotional regulation as a core target (our emotional regulation guide explains why that matters with ADHD).

It remembers. The listing says it learns your routines, sensitivities and goals over time, so you don't start from zero each day.

Where does Mindflow AI fall short?

Talking is not doing. A good conversation can end with a clear next step and still leave you frozen at the desk; that gap is task initiation, covered in our guide to task initiation deficit.

Its outcome claims are self-reported. A screenshot says 83% of members report improvement within a month, but the listing gives no study or method behind the number, so treat it as marketing, not evidence.

It is not care. Mindflow's own terms say it is not a provider of medical, mental health or therapy services and has not been evaluated by the FDA (Mindflow terms).

And it costs more than most planners: the cheapest listed monthly price is $17.99.

How much does Mindflow AI cost?

Mindflow is free to download, and its listing describes voice coaching, plans, streaks and insights as Premium features. The US App Store lists Premium at $17.99 a month and yearly plans at many price points from $29.99 to $119.99.

With that many listed prices, the offer you see likely depends on timing or promotions, so check the paywall before you subscribe.

Is Mindflow AI a replacement for an ADHD coach or therapist?

No. It can be a useful daily companion between appointments, but its own terms rule out medical or therapy use.
