What does Chillio look like in use?

The screenshots below are Chillio's official App Store images, not our own. We did not test Chillio hands-on, so we describe what the listing shows and what reviewers report.

Your day is a list of small goals, such as "Read 1 page of a book" or "Take 3 deep breaths." Each one you tick earns 10 coins and XP, and a growth screen tracks your level and your streak.

Reviewers also describe a short wellbeing lesson each morning, a house cleaning track that sorts chores into daily, weekly and monthly, and a chat with Chi, the cat. The App Store's latest update notes mention improvements to that chat.

Fans like how little they have to decide. "The daily lesson just appears, you don't have to choose it or plan for it," one wrote, and another praised "no streaks shaming, no aggressive notifications."

How much does Chillio cost?

Inside the app, Chillio Premium costs $19.99 a month, $39.99 every three months or $89.99 a year, according to its US App Store listing. The in-app purchase list also shows other price points from $3.99 to $49.99, and the listing does not say which offer you will see.

Many people sign up through the quiz on Chillio's website instead. Its terms say a first-period discount renews at the full price, and reviewers describe paid extras on the checkout screens, such as an AI add-on near $40 and downloadable PDFs.

Chillio's own refund terms call those add-ons final and non-refundable. If you take the quiz, read each screen before you tap the big button.

How do you cancel Chillio and get a refund?

It depends on where you paid. Check your App Store or Google Play subscriptions first, then your email for a receipt from Chillio's website.

  • Paid in the App Store: cancel in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, as described in Apple's cancellation guide. Refunds go through Apple's refund request page, not Chillio.
  • Paid on Google Play: cancel under Payments and subscriptions in the Play Store, per Google's help page.
  • Paid on get-chillio.app: deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel in the account settings or email support@get-chillio.app from the address you paid with, at least 24 hours before the period ends, per Chillio's web subscription terms.

Web payments are "non-refundable" except under a money-back guarantee with strict conditions. You must ask within 30 days, have followed the program for 6 days in a row (4 for weekly plans), and send screenshots of completed lessons as proof.

The same terms say the guarantee does not cover "I didn't like it" or "I didn't expect to be charged." Several App Store reviewers say they were offered a 30% or 50% partial refund instead.

Is Chillio legit?

Yes, it is a real, actively updated app (version 1.4.3, updated October 26, 2026) with thousands of ratings, not a fake listing. The "scam" label people search for comes from its sales tactics, not from the app failing to exist.

Know who you pay: the App Store seller is COREMOVE LIMITED in Cyprus, while the web terms name Digitaloom Inc in Delaware. And a 4.6 App Store rating sits next to 1.2 on Google Play, so the average hides a lot of disappointed buyers.

Is Chillio good for ADHD?

Partly. Its App Store description never mentions ADHD, but some of its ads and quiz funnels target ADHD and executive dysfunction, and a few reviewers with ADHD say it helped.

What it gets right is low friction. One small habit at a time, a lesson that appears without planning, and no shaming for broken streaks all fit how executive function, the brain's self-management system for planning and starting tasks, tends to struggle in ADHD.

What it doesn't do is help you start a task you're stuck on. Ticking off "brush your teeth" is a habit tracker's job, while a task initiation problem needs the first step made smaller (see our guide to task initiation deficit).

Chillio's site says it draws on CBT and habit research but names no study or clinician, and its terms say it is not a medical product. For ADHD treatment, see the NIMH ADHD overview.

Who should try Chillio?

Try Chillio if:

  • You like small daily habits and a short lesson to start the morning.
  • A cute character and no streak shaming help you come back each day.

Skip it if:

  • You want an app that builds your plan for you. Several reviewers say they had to set up every task by hand.
  • Checkout screens with extra offers are the kind of thing you click through on autopilot. Surprise charges are a classic ADHD tax.

FAQ

Is the Chillio app a scam?

No, it is a real app with 4,112 US App Store ratings and regular updates. Many 1-star reviewers call it a scam because of paid add-ons at checkout, ads that don't match the app, and refunds that are hard to get.

How much does Chillio cost?

The US App Store lists Premium at $19.99 a month, $39.99 every three months or $89.99 a year. Web sign-ups often start with a discounted first period that renews at full price, plus optional paid add-ons.

How do I cancel my Chillio subscription?

If you paid through Apple or Google, cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. If you paid on get-chillio.app, cancel in the account settings or email support@get-chillio.app at least 24 hours before renewal; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund from Chillio?

App Store purchases are refunded through Apple, not Chillio. Web purchases are non-refundable except under a money-back guarantee that requires asking within 30 days and proving you followed the program for 6 days in a row.

Is Chillio good for ADHD?

It can help with small daily habits and chores, and some reviewers with ADHD like its low pressure. It does not plan your day or help you start a task you're stuck on, and it is not a medical product.
