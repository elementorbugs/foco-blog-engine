What does Structured look like in use?

The screens below come from Structured's official App Store screenshots, not our own. We did not test Structured hands-on, so we describe what the listing shows and what Structured's help center says.

Your day is a vertical timeline. Each task is a colored block whose size matches how long it takes, so "Yoga, 30 min" visibly takes more room than "Coffee, 15 min."

Tasks without a time sit in an inbox until you drag them into the day. Widgets and Live Activities show what's on now and what's next, and the latest update added a focus timer.

With Pro, you can type or say something like "reschedule my unfinished tasks from today to tomorrow," and Structured AI drafts or moves the blocks. Replan does the same for missed tasks.

Reviewers often praise how much is free and how it suits ADHD. One calls it an "outstanding planner" with "amazing attention to details that work well for ADHD brains," and a student "downloaded like 6 different planning apps" and "deleted all but this one."

The complaints are mostly about friction. One 4-star reviewer says "there are just too many mouse clicks involved in creating a task," and others want longer task durations or easier dragging.

The catch for ADHD is recurring tasks. Daily routines like meds, meals or a morning reset are exactly what many people want to repeat, and on the free plan you add them by hand.

How much does Structured Pro cost?

Structured sells Pro monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime purchase, and its help center says prices vary by region. The US App Store listing shows monthly options of $2.99 and $6.99, yearly options from $9.99 to $49.99, and lifetime at $99.99.

The listing doesn't say which offer you will see, so check the price on the paywall before you pay. Because Pro is bought in the app, you cancel or request refunds through the App Store or Google Play, not through Structured.

The lifetime option is rare among planners, and it is worth considering if you know you'll use Structured for years. One payment also removes the risk of a renewal you forget about.

Is Structured good for ADHD?

For many people, yes. Its App Store description says it is "built for ADHD minds," and the visual timeline speaks directly to time blindness, the difficulty of sensing how long things take and how much time is left (see our guide to time blindness and ADHD).

Seeing a task as a block with a start and an end turns "later" into a place on the screen. The Energy Monitor adds a rough view of how heavy the day is, which helps you avoid stacking demanding blocks back to back.

Structured doesn't claim research behind its design, and it isn't a treatment. For how ADHD is diagnosed and treated, see the NIMH ADHD overview and CHADD.

The gap is starting. A beautifully planned 9:00 block doesn't help if you sit frozen at 9:05, which is a task initiation problem, not a planning one (see our guide to task initiation deficit).

Psychologist Russell Barkley describes ADHD as a problem of doing what you know more than knowing what to do (Barkley, executive function fact sheet). A planner shows the plan; it can't make the first step feel smaller.

Where does Structured fall short?

The Apple version is the full product, and other platforms trail it. Per Structured's own feature table, Android lacks Timeline Views and Replan, and the web app has no widgets, Energy Monitor, notifications or AI (Structured feature availability).

It is also a single-person planner. There is no way to share a schedule with a partner or family, which matters if your day depends on other people's plans.

Finally, it plans but doesn't coach. It won't break "write the report" into steps or stay with you while you do them.

FAQ

Is the Structured app free?

Mostly. The timeline, inbox, widgets, Energy Monitor and sync are free with no ads; Pro adds Structured AI, recurring tasks, calendar import, Replan and custom notifications.

How much does Structured Pro cost?

Prices vary by region. The US App Store lists monthly options of $2.99 and $6.99, yearly options from $9.99 to $49.99, and a $99.99 lifetime purchase.

Is Structured good for ADHD?

Many people with ADHD like it because the visual timeline makes time easier to see. It helps you plan the day, but it doesn't help you start a task you're stuck on.

Does Structured work on Android and the web?

Yes, but with fewer features. Android lacks Timeline Views and Replan, and the web app has no widgets, notifications or AI.

Can you share a Structured schedule?

No. Structured is a planner for one person, with no shared or family schedules.

Who makes Structured?

Structured is made by unorderly GmbH, a company based in Germany.
