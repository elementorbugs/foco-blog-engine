Short answer: Tiimo is the better app for planning your whole day visually, with a color-coded timeline, routines, Apple Watch and Mac support, and a much larger user base. FOCO is narrower: it is built for the moment you can't start one task, breaking it into timed steps and staying with you through step one.

  • Pick Tiimo if you need to see the shape of your day, want routines, or live on Apple Watch and Mac.
  • Pick Tiimo if you want a free version to start with.
  • Pick FOCO if the plan is clear but you still freeze, and you want small timed steps, a focus timer and a companion on screen.

Which is better when you can't start a task?

FOCO, for most people with that specific problem. Task initiation, the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it, is the one job FOCO is designed around.

Psychologist Russell Barkley describes ADHD as a problem of self-regulation more than knowledge (Barkley, executive function fact sheet). That is why a plan can be perfectly clear and still not get started.

FOCO asks for very little before step one: type or say the task, get timed steps, start a session. You can also capture a task by voice or photograph a handwritten list.

Tiimo can help with starting too, through its focus timer and task breakdown. Its order is different, though: you plan the day first, then work from the plan.

If building a schedule is itself the thing you avoid, that order can work against you. Our guide to task initiation deficit explains why.

Which is better for planning your whole day?

Tiimo, clearly. It is a dedicated visual planner, and FOCO is not trying to be one.

Tiimo puts tasks on a color-coded timeline with icons and supports repeating routines. For people with time blindness, seeing the day laid out can help more than another list.

FOCO does have a built-in calendar with morning, afternoon and anytime sections, reminders, and missed tasks that roll forward instead of piling up as a red overdue wall. It is a simple day view, not a visual timeline.

Which works on more devices?

Tiimo, by a wide margin. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Android, Mac and the web, though the web app needs Pro (Tiimo pricing).

One caveat: Tiimo rebuilt its Android app from scratch in 2026 and says it is still adding features to match iOS (Tiimo's Android relaunch post). FOCO runs on iPhone and Android, with no web app and no watch app.

What does each one cost?

Tiimo: there is a free version with a selection of planning tools, and Pro is billed monthly or yearly. Tiimo does not publish one fixed price because it varies by country, and the 7-day free trial applies to the yearly plan only.

FOCO: a 7-day free trial, then $9 a month or $59 a year. There is no free tier, so after the trial it is a paid app.

If cost is the deciding factor, Tiimo's free version is the cheaper place to start. Check the store or the official site before you pay, since prices change.

Who should pick which?

Choose Tiimo if:

  • Text to-do lists never stick, but seeing your day laid out does.
  • You want routines and reminders across iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac.
  • You want to start free and only pay if the app earns it.
  • You will open it every day to plan.

Choose FOCO if:

  • You know what to do but can't start step one.
  • Setting up a planner is where you usually quit.
  • You want each step timed and a calm companion on screen while you work.
  • You are fine with a paid app after a 7-day free trial ($9 a month or $59 a year), on iPhone or Android.

FAQ

Is Tiimo free?

Tiimo has a free version with some planning tools on iOS and Android. The full app, including the web version, needs Pro, with a 7-day trial on the yearly plan.

How much is Tiimo Pro?

Tiimo bills Pro monthly or yearly and shows the price at checkout, because it varies by country. Check Tiimo's pricing page for your region.

Is FOCO free?

No. FOCO has a 7-day free trial, then costs $9 a month or $59 a year.

Is FOCO a replacement for Tiimo?

Not for visual day planning. FOCO solves a narrower problem, starting one stuck task, while Tiimo plans the whole day.

Can I use FOCO and Tiimo together?

Yes. Some people plan the week in Tiimo and open FOCO for the one task that won't start, since the two do not integrate but rarely overlap.

Is Tiimo on Android?

Yes. Tiimo relaunched a rebuilt Android app in 2026 and says it is still adding features to match the iPhone version.
