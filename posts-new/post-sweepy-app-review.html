Sweepy App Review: Cleaning Schedule for ADHD Homes

Sweepy is a home cleaning app for iPhone, iPad and Android that shows how dirty each room is, picks what to clean next, and turns chores into a points game for your household. Listing your chores is free, while the daily schedule and family leaderboard need a premium subscription.

  • Try Sweepy if you lose track of what needs cleaning, and seeing rooms turn red helps you choose a task.
  • Skip it if streaks and red warnings make you feel worse, or setting up every chore sounds like a chore itself.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if you know what to clean but cannot start, and you want the job split into small timed steps.

What does Sweepy look like in use?

We did not test Sweepy hands-on. The screens below are its official App Store screenshots, so they show the app as the developer presents it.

Each room gets a bar that goes from green to red as chores come due, with labels like "It's shiny clean!" and "Get down to work!" You add chores with how often they repeat, and Sweepy tracks when each was last done.

A Rooms screen sums up the house, with your streak and wildcards on top. Widgets put today's tasks on your home screen, and on iPad the schedule can be split per household member.

Is Sweepy good for ADHD?

It can be. Apple's own editors put it in an App Store story called "Manage ADHD With These Helpful Apps," and a few of its features fit how ADHD tends to work.

  • It answers "what now?": Red bars show what needs doing, so you do not have to hold the whole house in your head. That leans less on working memory.
  • It matches your energy: Tasks carry 1 to 3 points, and you can filter by effort, so a tired day can still be a "dust surfaces" day.
  • It shares the load: Other people in the house get their own tasks, which can cut down on reminding and nagging.

The catch is that Sweepy tells you what to clean, not how to start. If you stare at "Sweep and mop floor" and freeze, that is task initiation, which our guide to task initiation deficit explains.

ADHD is linked to differences in executive function, the skills behind planning and getting started, as Russell Barkley's fact sheet explains. For ADHD itself, the National Institute of Mental Health is a better place to start.

Where might Sweepy fall short?

Setup takes effort: You list every chore and how often it repeats before the bars mean anything. Sweepy offers task suggestions to speed this up.

The best parts are premium: The automatic daily schedule and the family game sit behind the subscription, according to its website and App Store screenshots.

Red can mean shame: A house full of red bars after a hard week can feel like a report card. If that happens, our ADHD cleaning planner uses a bare minimum list instead.

Streaks can backfire: Streaks motivate some people and make others quit after one missed day, though Sweepy adds "wildcards" that it shows next to the streak.

No help mid-task: Sweepy has no timer, no steps inside a chore, and no company while you clean.

How is Sweepy different from Tody?

Tody is the closest rival, and the App Store lists it right next to Sweepy. Both track rooms with bars that fill as chores come due.

Tody has been on the App Store since 2013 and holds 4.8 stars from 10,902 US ratings, against Sweepy's 4.7 from 10,783. Sweepy's own pitch leans on the household game, with points, approvals and a leaderboard, so compare both listings before you pick one.

FAQ

Is Sweepy free?

Partly. You can track rooms and add as many custom tasks as you want for free, while the daily schedule and household game need a premium subscription.

What does Sweepy Premium add?

Premium generates a daily cleaning schedule for each person in your home and unlocks the family competition. It is a monthly or yearly subscription with a 3-day trial.

Is Sweepy good for ADHD?

It can help with deciding what to clean and sharing chores, and Apple featured it in a story about ADHD apps. It does not help you start a task, so pair it with another tool if that is the hard part.

Does Sweepy work on Android?

Yes. Sweepy is on Google Play with 1M+ downloads and a 4.5-star rating, as well as on iPhone and iPad.

Is Sweepy better than Tody?

They are close. Both track rooms with bars and have similar US App Store ratings, and Sweepy leans into points and a family leaderboard.

Can kids use Sweepy?

Yes. You can add household members, and kids' tasks can be set to count only after a parent approves them.
