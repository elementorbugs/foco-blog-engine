What does Tiimo look like in use?

The screens below come from Tiimo's official App Store screenshots, not our own. We did not test Tiimo hands-on, so we describe what the listing shows and what Tiimo says the app does.

Your day is split into Anytime, Morning, Day and Evening, with each task as a soft, colored card. It feels calmer than a dense calendar grid.

The AI co-planner takes a messy message like "I need to call my mom, eat breakfast, bike to work and pick up my kids" and turns it into scheduled tasks. Tiimo says it can also break tasks into steps and estimate time (Tiimo AI planning).

When a task starts, a circular countdown shows the time draining away, with optional lo-fi music. Reminders, Live Activities, Apple Watch complications and mood check-ins round it out.

What do Tiimo users say?

The overall rating is high: 81% of Tiimo's US App Store ratings are 5 stars and 4.5% are 1 star. Recent written reviews are much more mixed.

We read the 250 most recent US App Store reviews that Apple's public review feed returned, posted December 12, 2025 to September 28, 2026. Of those, 128 gave 4 or 5 stars, 27 gave 3 stars and 95 gave 1 or 2 stars.

People with ADHD are its happiest users. "As a therapist with ADHD, I really love this app," one wrote, and another said it "does a great job of giving a visual representation of time."

The complaints cluster around money and calendars. Reviewers call it "massively overpriced," describe being charged after a trial, and say there is "no way to connect an outlook calendar."

The AI splits opinion. Some call it "excellent AI assistance," while others say it "cannot actually parse a slightly nuanced todo."

How much does Tiimo cost?

Tiimo's FAQ doesn't publish prices, and the US App Store listing shows Pro options from $7 to $48 without saying which is monthly or yearly. Prices also differ by where you pay.

When Daring Fireball reviewed it in December 2025, in-app Pro cost $12 a month or $54 a year, while the web offered $10 a month or $42 a year (Daring Fireball). Check the paywall for today's price.

Two billing rules matter. Per Tiimo's FAQ, the 7-day free trial applies only to the yearly plan, and monthly is charged as soon as you subscribe.

Several reviewers say they were charged for a full year after the trial. If you start one, put the end date in your calendar the same day; forgotten trials are a classic ADHD tax.

Is Tiimo free?

Partly. The free version is a "basic planner" with limited AI chats and one profile, and reviewers say much of the daily planning works without paying.

Calendar sync, unlimited AI, extra profiles, cross-device sync and the web app are Pro. If you want your Google or iCloud events in Tiimo, plan on paying.

Is Tiimo good for ADHD?

It is one of the few planners designed for it. Tiimo says it was "co-designed with ADHD/Autism experts," began as a university research project in Copenhagen, and its listing names time agnosia, the difficulty of sensing time passing, as a problem it targets.

The countdown timer is the clearest fit. Seeing time shrink on screen makes "how long is left" concrete, which helps with time blindness.

The calm, sectioned day also lowers the overwhelm of a full calendar. That matters because executive function, the brain's self-management system for planning and starting tasks, is under strain in ADHD (see our guide to ADHD and executive function).

Being well designed isn't the same as being proven. We found no trial testing whether Tiimo improves ADHD outcomes, and it isn't a treatment; for that, see the NIMH ADHD overview and CHADD.

And a plan still has to be started. If the Morning section arrives and you freeze, that is a task initiation problem (see our guide to task initiation deficit).

Where does Tiimo fall short?

Calendars are the biggest gap. Google and iCloud calendars can be imported, but there is no direct Outlook connection, and reviewers keep asking for two-way sync.

Tiimo also removed its dedicated routines feature in 2024 and folded it into tasks with subtasks, which some long-time users still miss. Android was rebuilt from scratch in May 2026, so it is catching up with the iPhone version (Tiimo Android relaunch).

Finally, there is no native Mac app, and the web app needs Pro.

Tiimo vs Structured: which is better?

They are the two best-known visual planners. Structured is mostly free with a single clean timeline, while Tiimo is softer, more ADHD-specific and leans on its AI co-planner and countdown timer.

FAQ

Is Tiimo free?

There is a free version with a basic planner, limited AI chats and one profile. Calendar sync, unlimited AI, more profiles and the web app need Pro.

How much does Tiimo cost?

Tiimo doesn't publish prices on its FAQ, and the US App Store lists Pro options from $7 to $48. In December 2025, Daring Fireball reported $12 a month or $54 a year in the app, and $10 or $42 on the web.

Does Tiimo have a free trial?

Yes, 7 days, but only on the yearly plan. The monthly plan is charged right away.

Is Tiimo good for ADHD?

Many people with ADHD like its visual timeline and countdown timer, and it was co-designed with ADHD and autism experts. It helps you plan and see time, but it doesn't make starting a stuck task easier.

Does Tiimo sync with Outlook?

Not directly. Tiimo connects to Google and iCloud calendars, and Outlook users report having to route it through Google.

Why did Tiimo win App of the Year?

Apple named Tiimo its iPhone App of the Year for 2025, recognizing its design for neurodivergent planning. It was also an Apple Design Awards finalist in 2024.
