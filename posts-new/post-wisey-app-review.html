What does Wisey look like in use?

The screenshots below are Wisey's official App Store images, not our own. We did not test Wisey hands-on, so we describe what the listing shows and what Wisey says the app does.

The core is a guided daily plan built from short courses, such as "Win Your Emotional Battle Against Procrastination" and "How to Make Rational Decisions." Wisey's web pages describe lessons of up to 20 minutes a day.

Around the lessons sit practical tools. There is a Pomodoro-style focus timer (25 minutes on, 5 off by default), an app blocker that asks "Are you sure you want to spend it here?", and an AI productivity coach.

Soundscapes mix music with rain, thunder or a bonfire, and a habit tracker shows a yearly grid. If timed work sessions are new to you, our guide to Pomodoro for ADHD explains how to adapt them.

Written reviews tell a sharper story than the stars. Of the 10 reviews on Wisey's US App Store page, 7 were about charges, refunds or the word "scam," and 5 were about cancelling.

"I agreed to around a $10 one time fee for a 28-day plan... Now suddenly I'm slapped with a $50 fee," one 1-star reviewer wrote. Another said they were "charged $99 for auto renewal" two months later, while a 2-star reviewer still liked "the lesson plan they give you and how personalized it is."

On Trustpilot, Wisey shows a TrustScore of about 4.0 from roughly 5,700 reviews, and the company replies to nearly all negative ones (Trustpilot). The repeated complaints are billing after cancelling, surprise renewals and an add-on workbook people say they didn't knowingly buy.

How much does Wisey cost?

There is no free version. In the app, the US App Store listing shows "Premium" purchases at $9.99 and $99.99, and Google Play lists in-app items from $9.99 to $99.99.

On the web, the plans set up in Wisey's checkout start low and renew higher. The ones we found were $9.99 for the first 28 days, then $48.50 every 28 days, or $18.99 for 84 days, then $79.99 every 84 days.

Billing every 28 days means 13 charges a year, not 12. At $48.50, that adds up to about $630 a year.

Two more lines in Wisey's terms are worth knowing. Add-ons offered at checkout are non-refundable, and if a renewal is declined, Wisey "will make several attempts to process the renewal using progressively smaller amounts" (Wisey terms of use).

How do you cancel Wisey and get a refund?

It depends on where you paid. Check your App Store or Google Play subscriptions first, then your email for a receipt from wisey.app.

  • Paid in the App Store: cancel in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, as in Apple's cancellation guide. Refunds go through Apple's refund request page.
  • Paid on Google Play: cancel under Payments and subscriptions in the Play Store, per Google's help page.
  • Paid on wisey.app: Apple and Google can't cancel it, and deleting the app doesn't either. Cancel in your Wisey account settings or email support@wisey.app before the period ends.

Web purchases are non-refundable except under a money-back guarantee, if one was offered at checkout. You must ask within 30 days and have "adhered to the plan for at least 14 days in a row," with screenshots of your plan history as proof (Wisey refund policy).

Fourteen days in a row is a high bar for anyone with ADHD. If you sign up, put the renewal date in your calendar the same day.

Is Wisey legit or a scam?

It is a real, working app with 878 App Store ratings and 50,000+ Google Play downloads, not a fake. The "scam" searches come from how it bills, not from the app failing to deliver anything.

Know who you pay: the current terms name Koflimin Limited in Cyprus, an archived version of the ADHD quiz site named GrowthMind Labs Limited in Las Vegas, and the terms say Wisey runs "several brand names for the same underlying products."

One reviewer also said the first charge showed up under a name they didn't recognize.

Is Wisey good for ADHD?

Partly. Its App Store description never mentions ADHD, and its lessons are general self-improvement, but its main sign-up funnel is an "ADHD test" that ends in a personal plan.

That quiz is a marketing tool, not an assessment, and its own fine print says it is not a formal diagnosis. A real ADHD diagnosis comes from a clinician, as CHADD's guide to diagnosing ADHD explains.

The tools do fit common ADHD friction. A focus timer, an app blocker and background sound address distraction and time blindness, and short lessons are easier to finish than long courses.

What lessons can't fix is task initiation, the step from knowing what to do to starting it (see our guide to task initiation deficit). Wisey names no clinician or study behind its plan; for ADHD treatment, see the NIMH ADHD overview.

FAQ

Is the Wisey app a scam?

No, it is a real app with 878 US App Store ratings and 50,000+ Google Play downloads. Many 1-star reviewers call it a scam because a low intro price renews at about $48.50 every 28 days, and refunds are hard to get.

How much does Wisey cost?

Web plans we found start at $9.99 for 28 days, then renew at $48.50 every 28 days, or $18.99 for 84 days, then $79.99 every 84 days. In the US App Store, "Premium" is listed at $9.99 and $99.99.

How do I cancel my Wisey subscription?

If you paid through Apple or Google, cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. If you paid on wisey.app, cancel in your Wisey account settings or email support@wisey.app; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund from Wisey?

App Store purchases are refunded through Apple. Web purchases are non-refundable unless you qualify for the money-back guarantee: ask within 30 days and show you followed the plan for at least 14 days in a row.

Is the Wisey ADHD test accurate?

No online quiz can diagnose ADHD, and Wisey's says it is not a formal diagnosis. It works as a sign-up funnel; a clinician makes the diagnosis.

Who makes Wisey?

The App Store seller and current terms name Koflimin Limited, based in Nicosia, Cyprus. Support is support@wisey.app.
