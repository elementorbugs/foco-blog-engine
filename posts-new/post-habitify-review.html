Habitify Review: Is This Habit Tracker App Good for ADHD?

Habitify is a habit tracker app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android that logs habits with reminders, streaks and charts, and can log some habits for you from Apple Health or Screen Time. The free plan tracks 3 habits, Plus is US$29.99 a year, and Pro adds Off Mode, timers and integrations.

  • Try Habitify if you want a clean tracker for a few daily habits, on Apple devices or Android.
  • Skip it if a broken streak tends to make you quit, unless you plan to use skips or Pro's Off Mode.
  • Pick FOCO, the app we make, if the habit is clear but starting it is the hard part, and you want it split into small timed steps.

What does Habitify look like in use?

We did not test Habitify hands-on. The screens below are its official App Store screenshots, so they show the app as the developer presents it.

The main screen is a journal: today's habits grouped by morning, afternoon and evening, each with a quick check-in, a counter or a timer. Reminders can fire at a time or, as one screenshot shows, when you arrive at a place.

Each habit has its own page with a calendar, streak history and weekly averages. On iPad and Mac, a sidebar with your areas and progress sits next to the journal.

Is Habitify good for ADHD?

It can be, if you keep it small. Habit apps rely on doing the same thing often, and ADHD is linked to differences in executive function, the skills behind planning and follow-through, as Russell Barkley's fact sheet explains.

Three things in Habitify fit that well:

  • Skips and Off Mode: You can mark a day skipped, and Pro can pause a habit during a sick week or a trip. The App Store listing puts it simply: "Your habits don't reset after one bad day."
  • Automatic logging: Pro can log walks, sleep or screen time from other apps, so there is one less thing to remember.
  • Location reminders: A reminder that fires when you get home may land better than one at a fixed time, if time blindness is part of your day.

Missing a day is normal. In a University College London study, missing one opportunity "did not significantly impact the habit formation process," though people who were very inconsistent did not form the habit.

It is a tracker, not a treatment. For questions about ADHD itself, the National Institute of Mental Health is a better place to start.

Where might Habitify fall short?

Three habits on the free plan: That is enough to start, but you will need Plus or Pro to track more.

The kindest features cost the most: Off Mode, automatic logging and the Pomodoro timer are Pro features, not Plus.

Streaks can backfire: Charts and streak counts motivate some people and shame others. Our printable ADHD habit tracker counts days instead of chains, if streaks get to you.

Reminders become noise: A dozen habits with a dozen reminders is easy to set up and easy to start ignoring. Start with fewer.

Tracking is not starting: A checkbox records a habit you did. It does not help when you are stuck at the start, which is task initiation, a separate problem.

How much does Habitify cost?

Habitify moved to three plans on April 6, 2026: Free, Plus and Pro, according to its changelog. Plus is US$29.99 a year on the US App Store and comes only as a yearly plan.

Pro comes monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime purchase, with a 7-day free trial on the App Store. Habitify's website pricing page still shows the older Premium plan at US$49.99 a year or US$119.99 once, and the changelog says Premium members were moved to Pro.

The App Store did not show Pro's price on the day we checked, so check the price in the app before the trial ends.

How is Habitify different from Habitica, Finch and Routinery?

Habitify is a plain tracker with charts. Habitica turns habits into a role-playing game, Finch ties self-care to a pet you look after, and Routinery runs a routine step by step with a timer.

If you want a game, Habitica or Finch may hold your interest longer. If you want a quiet log you check once a day, Habitify is closer to that.

FAQ

Is Habitify free?

Yes, the free plan tracks up to 3 active habits with 1 reminder per habit. Unlimited habits need Plus or Pro.

How much is Habitify Pro?

Pro comes monthly, yearly or as a lifetime purchase, with a 7-day free trial on the App Store. Habitify's website lists the older Premium plan, now Pro, at US$49.99 a year or US$119.99 once, so confirm the price in the app before you pay.

What is the difference between Habitify Plus and Pro?

Plus removes the limits on habits, reminders and areas, and is yearly only. Pro adds Off Mode, checklists, Pomodoro and stopwatch timers, calendar and health sync, Screen Time and RescueTime, Zapier, IFTTT and API access.

Does Habitify work on Android?

Yes. Habitify is on Google Play with 500K+ downloads and a 4.2-star rating, and it syncs with the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch apps.

Is Habitify safe?

Its App Store privacy label lists usage and diagnostics data, marked as not linked to you. Its privacy policy says health data from integrations is not used for advertising, while it does use Meta tools to measure its own ads.

Is Habitify good for ADHD?

It can help if you track only a few habits and use skips or Off Mode on hard days. It records habits but does not help you start a task, so pair it with a tool for that if starting is the problem.
