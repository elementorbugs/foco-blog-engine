Which one is actually built for ADHD?

Tiimo. It is designed and marketed for neurodivergent people first, while Structured is a general planner that many ADHD users happen to love.

That difference matters less than it sounds. A tool built with you in mind is not automatically better than one that simply fits how your brain works.

Many people with ADHD do better with fewer choices on screen, because planning, prioritizing and holding steps in mind all lean on executive function. Structured's sparse layout can be easier to keep opening than a fuller app.

Which handles time blindness better?

Both, in slightly different ways. Time blindness, trouble sensing how much time has passed or how long something will take, is the problem both timelines are trying to make visible (our time blindness guide goes deeper).

Structured is the more direct answer: one line, one day, blocks sized to real duration. Its Energy Monitor, built with the Dutch institute Breinstraat, also lets you score tasks by how draining they are and warns you when a day is overloaded (Structured help: Energy Monitor).

Tiimo does the same timeline job with more scaffolding, including routines and a built-in focus timer. If you want help staying with a block once it starts, that extra layer earns its place.

Which works on my devices?

Both now work almost everywhere, so devices no longer decide this comparison. Structured used to be Apple-only, but it launched on Android in November 2023 and on the web in August 2024 (Structured help: platform availability).

Structured says some iOS features are still missing on Android and web, and that a small team is adding them gradually. Tiimo is in a similar spot: its Android app was rebuilt in 2026 and is still catching up to iOS (Tiimo's Android relaunch post).

If you live on an iPhone, both apps give you the full experience. On Android or a laptop browser, expect a slightly thinner version of either.

Which is cheaper?

Structured, for most people, because almost everything works without paying. Its help center says you can use "almost every feature of Structured for free," with Pro adding Structured AI, recurring tasks, calendar import and custom notifications (What is Structured Pro?).

Structured Pro comes as monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase, and Structured says the price depends on your region and currency. Tiimo Pro is monthly or yearly, the price shown at checkout varies by country, and the 7-day free trial applies to the yearly plan only (Tiimo pricing).

Neither company publishes one fixed price, so we are not quoting one. Check the in-app paywall in your country before you pay.

Does either app help you start a task you're avoiding?

Not really, and that is a different problem from planning. Both apps are good at showing the shape of a day you have already decided to do.

Neither is built for the moment of staring at a task and not being able to begin. That is task initiation, and our guide to task initiation deficit explains why a good plan alone often doesn't fix it.

Who should pick which?

Choose Tiimo if:

  • You want an app made for neurodivergent people, from onboarding to design.
  • You want routines and a focus timer next to the timeline.
  • You will use it daily, so a Pro subscription makes sense.

Choose Structured if:

  • You want the simplest possible timeline with little visual noise.
  • You want most features free, or a one-time lifetime option.
  • You tend to overbook yourself and want an energy check on your day.

Choose FOCO if:

  • Your day is planned, but you freeze at the first task.
  • You want AI to break a stuck task into small steps, each with a time estimate, then a focus timer with an on-screen companion.
  • You are fine with a paid app after a 7-day free trial ($9 a month or $59 a year), on iPhone or Android.

FAQ

Is Structured free?

Mostly. Structured says almost every feature is free, and Pro adds extras like Structured AI, recurring tasks and calendar import.

Is Tiimo free?

Tiimo has a free version with some planning tools on iOS and Android. The full app, including the web version, needs Pro, with a 7-day trial on the yearly plan.

Does Structured work on Android?

Yes. Structured launched on Android in November 2023 and on the web in August 2024, though some iOS features are still missing on both.

Is Structured cheaper than Tiimo?

Usually, because most of Structured is free and it offers a lifetime option. Both apps set Pro prices by region, so compare the paywalls in your own country.

Which app is better for autistic adults?

Tiimo is designed with autistic and ADHD users in mind. Many autistic people use Structured happily too, so the simpler screen may suit you better.

What is Structured's Energy Monitor?

It is an opt-in feature where you give each task energy points and Structured warns you when the day goes over your limit. It is not available on Structured Web yet.
