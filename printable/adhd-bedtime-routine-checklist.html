ADHD Bedtime Routine Checklist

14 small steps, split into wind down (calm your body and brain) and set up tomorrow (remove decisions before you're too tired to make them). Check off what you need tonight, skip the rest.

Wind Down
Set a bedtime alarm
Signals that it's time to start winding down
Put devices away
Reduces stimulation before sleep
Dim the lights
Cues your brain that night is coming
Take meds or supplements (if relevant)
Consistency matters more than timing
Wash up and brush teeth
A physical cue that the day is ending
Light stretch or relaxation
Releases physical tension from the day
Brain dump on paper
Empties racing thoughts before bed
Set Up Tomorrow
Lay out tomorrow's clothes
Removes a decision for tomorrow morning
Pack your bag
One less thing to think about tomorrow
Check tomorrow's calendar
Reduces morning surprises
Set out one visible reminder
Connects tonight to tomorrow's first step
Charge your phone away from bed
Reduces the temptation to scroll
Tidy one surface
A small win waiting for future you
Note one good thing about today
Ends the day on a steady note
