ADHD Morning Routine Checklist

14 small steps, split into foundations (do these first, body before brain) and momentum (once you're actually online). Check off what you need today, skip the rest.

Foundations
Wake up on time
A calmer start than a snoozed alarm
Drink water
Hydrate your brain before anything else
Take your meds (if relevant)
Set yourself up for the rest of the list
Move your body
Stretch, a short walk, or a few jumping jacks
Have a nourishing breakfast
Fuel your focus for the morning ahead
Set your top 3 priorities
Keep it to three, not a full to-do list
Check your calendar
Know what's ahead before it surprises you
Momentum
Do one small avoided task
Pick the tiniest version and build momentum
Read or learn something
Feed your brain before the day fills up
Focus session (10-25 min)
Progress over perfection, timer optional
Take a mindful moment
A few breaths before the momentum takes over
Set your intention for today
Finish the sentence: "Today I choose..."
Be kind to yourself
You're doing enough, even on a half-checked day
Let's do this
However far you got, the day is moving
