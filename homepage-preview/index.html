FAQ

Quick answers.

What is task paralysis?

Task paralysis is the gap between knowing what you need to do and being able to start it. You are not avoiding the task and you have not forgotten it. You simply cannot make the first move. It is commonly described alongside ADHD, where it overlaps with task initiation, a recognized executive function. Read the full breakdown of ADHD task paralysis.

Can an app actually help with task paralysis?

It can if it targets the right moment. Most productivity apps assume you can start, so they hand you a longer list. FOCO gives you one subtask at a time, never the whole task, and an AI focus companion stays present while you work it. That combination is aimed squarely at the start, not at the planning.

Is FOCO an ADHD planner app?

Yes. FOCO is an ADHD planner app with a built-in calendar and reminders, built around the hardest part: starting. You add tasks by voice, text or a photo of a handwritten list, and one tap breaks a task into small, timed steps.

Is this just another to-do app?

No. To-do apps assume you can start. FOCO assumes you can't, and solves that.

What if I've tried everything already?

That's exactly who FOCO is for. Most tools fail because they expect motivation. FOCO removes the need for it.

Do I need ADHD?

No. If you struggle to start, FOCO helps. Diagnosed or not.

What if I lose focus again?

You will. That's normal. FOCO is built for that. It gently brings you back, without guilt.
