      Questions about the body doubling room

      FOCO’s body doubling room is a free, live online room for people with ADHD. You sit down at a shared table with real people, say one thing you’re working on, pick 15 to 90 minutes and focus side by side. No camera, no account, no booking. When you want the same help outside the room, the FOCO app breaks your next task into tiny steps.

      What is a body doubling room?

      A body doubling room is an online space where you work while other people work beside you. FOCO’s room is free and live: you sit down at a shared table, say what you’re working on, pick 15 to 90 minutes and focus with everyone else who is there.

      Is it free?

      Yes. The room is free to use, with no sign up and no account. You pick a name and a seat picture, and that’s it.

      Do I need a camera or a microphone?

      No. Nobody sees or hears you. Other people see your seat picture, your first name and one line about your task, and you can send hearts and kind words from a fixed list.

      How long is a session?

      You choose: 15, 25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, followed by a short break of 5 to 15 minutes. Everyone keeps their own clock, so you can start whenever you sit down.

      How is it different from Focusmate?

      Focusmate pairs you with one partner on video for a booked slot. FOCO’s room is a shared table you can join any time, without video, booking or an account. If you want to start right now, with no video and nothing to book, the room is built for that.

      Does body doubling help with ADHD?

      Many people with ADHD say that working next to someone makes it easier to start and keep going. Research on body doubling itself is still limited, so treat it as a strategy to try and see how it feels for you.

      Is my information private?

      The room only keeps the name and the task line you type while you are seated, and removes them when you leave. Kind words disappear after an hour. There are no uploads, and people can hide anyone they don’t want to see.
