Why these help with ADHD.

Timers make time visible

Meta-analyses find people with ADHD estimate time less accurately on average (JAACAP, 2022). A visible countdown lets you check time instead of guessing. More on time blindness.

A brain dump frees your head

Holding every open loop in mind leans on working memory, one of the executive functions ADHD affects (NIMH). Writing them down puts them somewhere safe.

Company makes starting easier

Many people with ADHD find it easier to start and keep going when someone else is working nearby. That is the idea behind body doubling.
