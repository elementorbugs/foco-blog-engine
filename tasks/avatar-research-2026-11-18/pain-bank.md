# Pain bank (2026-11-18)

Every pain from the 5 reports in one table, with the FOCO answer that is TRUE (per `viral-lab/CREATIVE-RULES.md` section 1).
Verbatim quotes and their links are in report 01-05 (column "Src"). On screen: a quote with quote marks only if it was
copied exactly with its link; otherwise paraphrase in the character's voice without quote marks.

**What FOCO really does (use only these):**
- **SAY** = say / type / snap a task, no system to set up.
- **DAY** = it lands in your day (the calendar) in a second.
- **STEPS** = one tap breaks it into tiny steps, which are the instructions.
- **REMIND** = a reminder for a saved task.
- **WITH** = focus session: FOCO sits with you, timer + sounds.
- **REPLAN** = the AI plans and reschedules the day.

**FOCO does NOT:** resume a task after an interruption, sync with Google/Apple calendar, share with a partner, or do
the work for you.

Int. and Uns. are scored 1-5. Uns. = how unsolved the pain is. Freq. is the share of the hand-coded threads in that report.
