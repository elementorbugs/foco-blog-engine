Built from 5 parallel research passes. The detailed reports, quotes and links are in files 01-05 in this folder.
**Total:** about 9,000 unique posts and about 380 full threads read (Reddit, Mumsnet, Hacker News, ADDitude).
**Scoring:** Intensity and Unsolved are 1-5. Frequency is the share of the hand-coded threads.

## The common "boiling pain" across all avatars
Starting with no outside trigger. A client deadline or a boss's demand works; anything that depends only on them does not start.
- Freelancers: "I'm my own boss, and I don't respect the boss" / "I work best alone with instruction".
- Managers: "my boss has to turn the key for me to do anything".
- Crosscut: brute-force task starting gets the highest median upvotes (1,702).
- Tools that failed: Pomodoro, alarms, to-do apps, planners ("A NEW PLANNER WILL NOT CHANGE YOUR LIFE", 5,576).
- **What works (reported):** taking the decision out of the moment of starting. First steps written "like robot instructions", someone else demanding the work, a pre-agreed micro-day. That is FOCO's core mechanism.

## Key findings that change the messaging
1. **Moms describe time and interruptions, not "paralysis".** Small-window/nap mentions: 16% for moms vs 3% for non-parents. Paralysis wording: 11% vs 19%. → For moms the message is "you don't need 3 hours of hyperfocus, you need the next 10-minute step", not "stuck?".
2. **ADHD = shame toward herself; without ADHD = anger at others.** The mental load is equal (26% vs 25%), but the ADHD mom blames herself. FOCO's playground is the shame, not the resentment.
3. **The shame -> ghosting -> bigger shame chain** (freelancers 27%, freelance moms 45% of self-employed threads). What the community says works: a tiny first action, one honest line to the client.
4. **Money goes to people and services that take the load off, not to software.** Apps die once they need upkeep ("one more thing to manage"). → Onboarding must be zero-setup.
5. **The partner's workday is protected, hers absorbs the interruptions.**

## Recommendation
- **Core avatar:** women with ADHD who work on their own (freelance / WFH), with or without kids. Jess stays the face.
- **Two message variants:**
  - Freelancers: "What can be done any time never gets done. FOCO gives you the instructions."
  - Moms: "The window is short. FOCO gives you the next step that fits in it."
- **Secondary marketing angle:** late diagnosis / perimenopause: "an external brain when the meds stop holding."
- **Not now:** managers (requires external calendar sync), non-ADHD moms.

## Product changes the research justifies (by impact)
1. **"I'm back" after an interruption:** one tap returns you to the step where you stopped (the moms' gap, unsolved 5).
2. **Ready-made shame-free tasks:** "reply to a client I'm late on", "send an invoice", "chase a payment", each with a tiny first step.
3. **Steps sized to a window:** "I have 20 minutes" -> just the steps that fit.
4. **External calendar sync:** opens the door to employees and managers. Later.

## Data limits
- Mostly Reddit/US. Parent-freelancers are a small slice (14-203 posts depending on the definition).
- Theme counts come partly from keyword coding (±5 points).
- Mumsnet quotes came through a summarizing fetch tool: check against the source before public use.
- Not reached: Facebook groups, TeamBlind, Indie Hackers.
