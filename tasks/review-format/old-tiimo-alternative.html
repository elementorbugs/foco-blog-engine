What is the best Tiimo alternative for ADHD?

The best Tiimo alternative depends on what you actually want changed. Both apps break a task into steps, so it isn't a question of one planning and the other not.

The difference is what each one keeps in front of you. Tiimo lays out the whole task on a clear visual schedule. FOCO narrows you down to a single subtask at a time, and adds a companion character that responds to your focus.

So the honest question is: do you want to see the whole task, or be guided through it one small step at a time?

What is Tiimo good at?

Tiimo earns its reputation. It is built around clear, visual, color-coded timelines, and it can break a task into steps and lay them out across your day.

Its focus is the whole task in context: you see everything that's coming, in order, on a visual schedule. If your main struggle is seeing your day, remembering what's next, and building visible structure, Tiimo is a genuinely strong choice and we won't pretend otherwise. A good ADHD planning system matters.

The question is what happens when seeing the whole task is exactly what overwhelms you.

Where focusing on the whole task falls short

For a lot of ADHD adults, seeing the entire task, even neatly broken into steps, is the thing that triggers the freeze.

The full list is still visible, so the brain still registers "this is big," and the activation energy to begin stays high. That's the wall behind task paralysis: not a lack of structure, but too much of the task in view at once.

What helps many ADHD brains is the opposite of a full overview: shrinking the visible task down to just the next small piece, so there's only ever one thing to face.

How is FOCO different?

FOCO breaks a task into subtasks like Tiimo can, but it differs in two ways that matter for ADHD brains.

One subtask at a time, not the whole task. In FOCO's focus mode you see a single subtask, not the full list. You're only ever facing the next small step, which keeps the "this is too big" feeling from showing up at all. Finish one, the next appears.

A companion character that responds to you. On the focus screen, the FOCO character changes with your state. It's calm and present while you're focused, and it shifts when you pause the task or drift out of focus, so it feels like something is actually there with you, reacting to how the session is going.

What's the main difference between Tiimo and FOCO?

Both break a task into steps. Tiimo keeps the whole task in view on a visual schedule, while FOCO puts a single subtask in front of you at a time. FOCO also has a character that changes with your focus state, so it feels like a present companion rather than a timer.

Can I use a planner and FOCO together?

Yes, and many people do. Use a visual planner like Tiimo for the overview of your day, and use FOCO for the focused work itself, when you want to move through one step at a time without facing the whole task.

What is FOCO's focus mode?

The Bottom Line

Tiimo and FOCO both break tasks down, so this isn't planner versus no planner. It's whole-task focus versus one-subtask-at-a-time focus.

If seeing the entire task is what freezes you, the answer isn't a clearer overview. It's a tool that shows you only the next small step and keeps a companion present while you take it. That's what FOCO is built to do.
