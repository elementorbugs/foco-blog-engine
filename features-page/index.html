FAQ

Questions about the features.

What does the FOCO app do?

FOCO helps you start tasks you keep avoiding. You add a task by voice, text or a photo of a handwritten list, one tap breaks it into small timed steps, and focus mode with an AI companion stays on screen while you work through the first one. A built-in calendar, reminders and progress stats keep the rest in place.

Do I get every feature on both plans?

Yes. The monthly and yearly plans include the same features. The yearly plan is $59 a year and starts with a 7-day free trial; the monthly plan is $9 a month. See plans.

Is there a web version of FOCO?

No. FOCO is an app for iPhone, iPad and Android. The free tools on this website (timers, brain dump, body doubling room) run in any browser, but they are separate from the app.

Is FOCO built for ADHD or just marketed to it?

Built for it. Every feature targets task initiation, the step most ADHD tools skip. Read the founder story for where it came from.

How is FOCO different from Todoist or Things?

Those are task managers that assume you can start once a task is written down. FOCO assumes you can't, and puts its features at that exact moment: breaking the task down and staying with you through the first step.
