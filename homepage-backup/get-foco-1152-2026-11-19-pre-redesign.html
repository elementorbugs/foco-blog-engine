FAQ

Is FOCO available now?

Yes, on both platforms. FOCO is live on the App Store for iPhone and iPad and on Google Play for Android, and you can download it today.

Which iPhones and iPads does FOCO run on?

Any device running iOS 13 or later, which covers the iPhone 6s and newer as well as most iPads still receiving updates.

Will the free tools always be free?

Yes. The website tools, focus timers, brain dump, and body doubling, are free forever. The mobile app is $59/year with a 7-day free trial, or $9/month.

What does the app cost?

FOCO is $59/year with a 7-day free trial, or $9/month, on the App Store and Google Play. See current pricing →

Do I pay twice if I use both iPhone and Android?

App subscriptions are billed by whichever store you bought through, so start on the device you actually work from. If you need to move a subscription across platforms, email support@tryfoco.com and we will help.

Is FOCO right for me?

If you sit down to work and cannot start, yes. If you have a clear sense of what to do but your brain freezes in front of it, yes. If you already have working focus systems and just need another to-do list, there are better tools for you. Read about who FOCO is for →
