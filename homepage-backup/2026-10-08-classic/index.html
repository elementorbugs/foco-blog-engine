What task paralysis feels like

You have something to do.
But you don't start.

You sit down. You open your laptop. And then… nothing.
FOCO stays with you.
Until you can begin.

Not because you're lazy.

Not because you don't care.

Because your brain doesn't know where to begin.

20 minutes.

You sit there. Staring at the screen. Thinking. Not starting.

Anything else.

You switch tasks. Anything else suddenly feels easier than the thing you should do.

An hour gone.

"Just a second" on your phone. You look up, and the morning is gone.

Later.

Tomorrow. Next week. And eventually, never.

You're not broken. There's a name for this.

This is not a willpower problem

It has a name.

It's called
Task Initiation Deficit

Most people call it task paralysis. ADHD brains don't struggle with doing.
They struggle with starting.

That invisible moment between
"I should do this" → "I'm doing it"

That's where everything breaks.

The forms it takes

Task paralysis
has more than one shape.

Task paralysis is the gap between knowing what to do and being able to start it. FOCO is built for that exact gap, by handing you one subtask at a time instead of the whole task.
