# FOCO App Store screenshots: design brief (Adi, 2026-10-14)

Size: 1320x2868 (iPhone 6.9"). Built by `node store-screens/build.js`, output in `out/`.
Real app screenshots go in `raw/` (they override `assets/`).

## Rules
1. Brand colours only: near-black, purple, lilac, white. No orange.
2. Scan, text and audio all visibly end up in the calendar as a task.
3. One connected story across the slides (Brain.fm style).
4. Short, bold, simple text. No clutter.
5. Show the action: a real scanner (Cal AI corner brackets + beam) pulling tasks off paper.
6. Audio looks like a recorder (big waveform, recording state).
7. A female freelancer as the human thread.
8. Pop-out elements jumping out of the phone.
9. No prices, plans, paywall or "FREE" badges.
10. Phone screens are real screenshots or 1:1 rebuilds of real screens.

References: Cal AI, Brain.fm.
