# Video plan: one pain, one FOCO answer (2026-11-18)

Source: `tasks/avatar-research-2026-11-18/pain-bank.md` (P-numbers) + `00-synthesis.md`.
Rules: `viral-lab/CREATIVE-RULES.md` wins. Every video = ONE specific pain + ONE FOCO feature that answers it easily,
shown truthfully: SAY (say it out loud, no complicated system) · DAY (in the calendar in a second) · STEPS (one tap = instructions)
· REMIND · WITH (FOCO stays with you, timer + sounds).
Not repeated (already made): ours/paralysis, ours/waiting, ours/beforestart, ours/unstarted.

**The shared spine of every video:** the pain (in her/their words) -> "it's not laziness, it's [X]" -> the easy moment
with FOCO (3-6 s, real screen) -> a small win. FOCO comes in at the turn, not before.

---

## Series A: mascot list reels (third person, "people with ADHD", male narrator, 27-38 s)
Engine: `foco-video/src/listreels`. Official FOCO (composite), 3-4 Flash images, the rest in code.

### A1 "Any time = no time" (P1, freelancers) ★ priority 1
- **Hook:** "Nobody tells you this about working for yourself with ADHD: the work with no deadline is the work that never happens."
- **Body:**
  - A client deadline? Done at 2am, beautifully.
  - Their own invoice, their own website, the pitch they've meant to send since March? "Sometime." And sometime never comes.
  - They're their own boss, and the boss lets them get away with everything.
- **The turn (DAY + REMIND):** they say "send the pitch" -> Foco puts it at 10:00 tomorrow -> a reminder rings -> first step on screen.
- **End card:** Give "any time" a time.
- **FOCO state:** drift -> focus.

### A2 "They don't ghost because they don't care" (P3) ★ priority 2
- **Hook:** "People with ADHD don't ghost clients because they don't care. They ghost because they care too much."
- **Body:**
  - One late day becomes an email they're scared to open.
  - Every day the reply needs to be better, so it gets harder.
  - Now the inbox itself feels dangerous.
- **The turn (STEPS + WITH):** "Reply to Dana" -> one tap -> step 1: open the email. Step 2: write one honest sentence. Step 3: send. FOCO sits next to them with the timer.
- **End card:** One honest line first.
- **Note:** zero shame, the tone is tender.

### A3 "Great at hard work, bad at deciding what the work is" (P2/P5)
- **Hook:** "People with ADHD can do the hardest job in the room, as long as someone else tells them where to start."
- **Body:** give them a list and they fly; give them an open day and they freeze. That's why a 9-5 felt like a cage, and freelance feels like a fog.
- **The turn (STEPS):** "Redesign the website" -> one tap -> robot-simple instructions: open the laptop / open the file / change the headline.
- **End card:** Your instructions, one tap away.

### A4 "The important work happens at 11pm" (P13, managers)
- **Hook:** "Promoted people with ADHD have a secret: the most important work they do happens at 11pm."
- **Body:** all day meetings and Slack fires win, and the strategy "can wait till tomorrow". They're great in the room, and the doc stays empty.
- **The turn (DAY + STEPS + WITH):** "Q1 plan" -> 9:00 in the morning, before the first meeting -> step 1: write 3 bullets -> FOCO + music.
- **End card:** Book the thinking before the meetings book you.

### A5 "If you tell them in the hallway, it didn't happen" (P12)
- **Hook:** "If you tell someone with ADHD something in the hallway, it didn't happen. And it's not on purpose."
- **Body:** "Can you send me that deck?" Yes, of course. Gone by the elevator. They've built reminders to remind them to write down reminders.
- **The turn (SAY + DAY + REMIND):** they say it to Foco while walking, 3 seconds -> tomorrow 10:00 + a reminder.
- **End card:** Say it once. It stays.

### A6 "The hardest day is the empty one" (P15, non-parents)
- **Hook:** "The hardest day for people with ADHD isn't the busy one. It's the empty Saturday."
- **Body:** when someone needs them, they're a machine. When nobody's waiting, they sit on the couch for 20 minutes, anxiety climbing, and the day is gone.
- **The turn (DAY + WITH):** three tiny things in the day, and FOCO sits with them for the first one.
- **End card:** Someone to start with.
- **Note:** FOCO is an animated character, never "a real person".

### A7 "Nobody warned women with ADHD about their 40s" (P17) ⚠ needs Adi's approval
- **Hook:** "Nobody warned women with ADHD that their 40s might feel like starting over."
- **Body:** the systems that held for 20 years suddenly stop holding. Many women describe it exactly like that. It's not you getting lazy.
- **The turn (SAY + DAY):** not another system: just say it, and it's held.
- **Caution:** no medical claims, no "hormones cause". The ADDitude survey only in the caption, as self-report.

## Series B: Jess stories (first person, text-led, no narrator, a work task inside a kid window)
Engine: the carousel skill / Story video. **Story text goes to Adi for approval before images.**

### B1 "Nap time is 40 minutes" (P6) ★ priority 3 (the hottest mom pain)
1. 13:02. He's asleep. Do I have 20 minutes? 40?
2. 13:15. I've spent 13 of them deciding where to start.
3. I can't "get into it" in 40 minutes. I need 3 hours I don't have.
4. *(Turn)* I said to FOCO: logo revision for Dana. One tap.
5. Step 1: open the file. Step 2: change the font. Step 3: export.
6. 13:41. Three steps done. He woke up. (FOCO next to her)
- **End:** You don't need 3 hours. You need the next step.

### B2 "9 days" (P3)
1. Dana's email has been sitting there 9 days.
2. Every day the answer has to be better, so I don't open it.
3. *(Turn)* FOCO: "reply to Dana" -> step 1: one honest sentence.
4. Sent from the car at drop-off. She answered: no problem 🙂
- **End:** The first step is one sentence.

### B3 "The note in the backpack" (P9 + P19)
1. 7:40. A note in the backpack: pajama day on Thursday + bring $5.
2. I'll remember. (I won't remember.)
3. *(Turn)* Snap it -> it's in Thursday, with a reminder Wednesday night.
4. Thursday. He's in pajamas. Nobody knows how close it was.
- **End:** Snap it. Forget it. It remembers.
- **Truth:** a reminder exists. No family sharing; it's her capture.

### B4 "The invoices I didn't send" (P4)
1. End of the month. Three invoices I didn't send.
2. I did the work. I just... subcontract for free, apparently.
3. *(Turn)* "send invoices" -> one tap -> open the template / fill in Dana / send.
4. Done before pickup.
- **No amounts or prices on screen.**

## Series C: real screen explainers (15-30 s, real recordings only)
Engine: Remotion ClipDemo (vsl). Hand-made look, not AI (CREATIVE-RULES section 2).

### C1 "Instead of a complicated system, just say it" (P11) ★ priority 4
- **Hook (type-on):** The best ADHD system is the one you never have to maintain.
- Real recording: a voice note "call the accountant" -> lands in the day in a second -> one tap steps -> focus with FOCO + music.
- Handwritten notes: "no setup", "no columns", "1 sec". All 3 differentiators in 20 seconds.

### C2 "One task, start to finish" (P16)
- One real task (the same one on every screen): "Pitch for a new client". Say -> day -> steps -> start (focus) -> done.
- **Hook:** It's not a willpower problem. It's a missing first step.
