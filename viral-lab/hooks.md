## Opener formulas (Adi's list, 2026-10-27)
A 2-5 word spoken opener that opens a gap BEFORE the topic is named. They borrow curiosity, so line 2 must pay it off
by second 3 with an ADHD-specific paradox, or the viewer feels baited and leaves.

| Opener | Lever |
|---|---|
| Pause for a second. | command, pattern interrupt |
| Ever notice this pattern? | shared observation, "is it just me" |
| Here is the real truth. | hidden truth |
| Let me surprise you. | promised surprise |
| You need this now. | urgency + direct address |
| You may not agree with this. | contrarian, pulls comments |
| I just figured this out. | discovery, speaker as peer |
| Never start a ___ if you don't have these ___. | warning + list (retention to the end) |
| What would happen if... | hypothetical |
| I bet you didn't know this. | challenge, secret knowledge |

Rule: opener (1s) + topic-naming payoff (by 3s) + the payoff is a paradox or a call-out, never a definition.

### Taboo / confession family (Adi, 2026-10-27) - strongest form
| Opener | Lever |
|---|---|
| No one talks about this, but... | hidden truth, insider |
| This might trigger you, but... | warning, dare to keep watching |
| Nobody wants to admit this, but... | confession, "it's not just me" relief |
The built-in "but" glues the opener to the payoff in ONE breath, so the gap opens and closes inside 3s (no bait feel).
Payoff = a quiet truth people with ADHD recognise but feel ashamed of; deliver it with zero shame (validation, not mockery).
